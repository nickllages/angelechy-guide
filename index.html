    1 · Introduction, theory and meta

    1.3 How the deck works

    Angelechy is a chess-themed Fairy deck whose monsters all live in the Extra Deck. Your Spells and Traps do one of two things with them: Special Summon them, or place them face-up in your Spell & Trap Zone as Continuous Spells. A placed Angelechy fires one effect when it lands (a search, a send to the GY, or placing the next piece), then keeps a continuous effect for as long as it stays. Every Angelechy Monster Card on the field, summoned or placed, yours or theirs, also counts for Angelechy Verdict, which banishes your opponent's whole field at 5 or more.

    Two things to keep in mind from the start. A placed piece is a Continuous Spell, so Ghost Ogre and Typhoon can destroy it (its “when placed” effect still resolves), and Inspector Boarder doesn't count it as a monster card type. And the count Verdict reads is every Angelechy Monster Card on the field, on both sides and in both zones, including the Seneschal you give your opponent.

    Your 40 is half engine and half interaction. 20 engine cards build that board; 20 hand traps and Dominus Traps (23 interruptions counting Opening to e4) stop the opponent. Going first you build a board that limits what they can do. Going second you start building during their Standby Phase with Opening to e4 and spend hand traps on their key steps.

    What a finished board looks like

    Line A going first: Opposition gives them Seneschal and places Bastion, Bastion places Shatranga, Shatranga adds Verdict, and Problem summons Enlisted and places Destrier. Seneschal's trigger lets you summon a second Destrier as a body.

    How a game goes

    Going first

    1. Build to 4+ placed pieces with Verdict Set and a Dominus or Opening to e4 behind it (line A or B).
    2. Place Bastion early against destruction (Typhoon, Spell Shattering Sword, The Fallen & The Virtuous): it protects the others, not itself.
    3. On their turn, let them commit, then Verdict at 5+. Ghost Ogre on a piece doesn't stop its “when placed” effect.
    4. After siding: Inspector Boarder first (lines E and F). With no Ritual, Fusion, Synchro, Xyz, Pendulum or Link Monster on the field, they can't activate monster hand traps at all.

    Going second

    1. Their Standby Phase: Fuwalos first (it needs you to control no cards), then Opening to e4. With Castellan placed you can loop into a 2nd e4 (line C, EG1).
    2. Their Main Phase: hand traps on their key steps (each matchup page lists them). Monster hand traps first, Dominus from hand last.
    3. Your turn: Verdict at 5+ banishes their field; Kusanagi or Black-Winged Assault Dragon finish (lines D and G).

    Mistakes every new Angelechy player makes

    1. Dominus from hand first. Impulse from hand locks Ghost Ogre, Nibiru, Veiler, Fuwalos and Droll for the rest of the duel. One replay lost to Elfnote this way.
    2. e4 before Fuwalos. Once you control a card, Fuwalos and Purulia can't be activated.
    3. Counting only your side for Verdict. The Seneschal you gave them counts too.
    4. Opposition into Bastion when you still need a Spell. Place Destrier to search instead (DB Grinder).
    5. Synchros in Attack Position against ROLAD. Dark Charmer crashes into them; summon in Defense.
    6. Forgetting Seneschal has 3,000 ATK. With Boarder out it can attack over Boarder; line F sends it back.
    7. Not reading their cards. Opponents misread this deck and concede early; read their cards just as carefully. DB Grinder: “read the cards once a week”.

    Main deck · engine (20)

    Angelechy Opposition×3Your best card: one card makes two Angelechy cards

    Normal Spell · 1 activation per turn

    What it does
    Choose 2 Angelechy monsters in your Extra Deck whose names aren't already in your Spell & Trap Zone. Special Summon one to your opponent's Main Monster Zone and place the other in your Spell & Trap Zone. From the GY, banish it to shuffle up to 5 Angelechy cards from your GY or banishment back into the Deck (Extra Deck monsters go back to the Extra Deck). The turn you activate it, you can only Special Summon LIGHT or DARK Synchros from the Extra Deck.
    Why you play it
    It starts a combo by itself and isn't a search, so Ash, Droll and Purge can't touch it; of the common hand traps, only Impulse stops it. You usually give them Seneschal. Its mandatory trigger lets you summon a Fairy Synchro too (a negated Destrier works as a body), it counts for Verdict from their side, and because it's their monster effect during your Main Phase it turns on Triple Tactics Thrust. The GY effect refills your Verdicts and Disturbance.
    Why 3
    It's the starter you most want to open, and a second copy is next turn's play. Never side it out.
    Tips
    Place Destrier (search a Spell) rather than Bastion when you still need a Spell later (DB Grinder). Against Elfnote, put Seneschal in their center Main Monster Zone: Regina only summons from the Deck from the center (Lucas Sacco). Impulse negates the whole effect, so make them spend it on something else first when you can.
    Angelechy Problem×3Main starter: any Spell or Trap becomes Enlisted plus a piece

    Field Spell

    What it does
    Once per turn: discard a Spell or Trap to Special Summon a Level 2 Angelechy (Enlisted) from your Extra Deck and place an Angelechy. Also, when a face-up Angelechy Monster Card you control is destroyed, you can return a placed Angelechy to the Extra Deck and then Special Summon it.
    Why you play it
    One card and a spare become two pieces: usually Enlisted plus Castellan (send Verdict or Disturbance) or Destrier (search a Spell). Opening to e4 places it from the Deck, and Strategy Score and Terraforming fetch it. The once per turn belongs to each copy, so a second Problem gets its own use (line B).
    Why 3
    It's the starter most of your hands use.
    Tips
    Discard Verdict or Disturbance when you can, because their GY effects still work. Otherwise discard Endgame, a sided Thrust, or a Dominus you won't Set.
    Angelechy Strategy Score×3Searches whichever piece your hand is missing

    Normal Spell · each effect once per turn

    What it does
    Add any Angelechy Spell or Trap from your Deck except Strategy Score. For the rest of that turn you can only Special Summon LIGHT or DARK Synchros from the Extra Deck. On a later turn, from the GY: banish it to return a placed Angelechy to the Extra Deck, then you can Special Summon it.
    Why you play it
    It's a copy of Problem, Opposition, Opening to e4 or Verdict, whichever you need.
    Why 3

    Tips

    Foolish Burial Goods×3A starter through Disturbance or Verdict in the GY

    Normal Spell · 1 per turn

    What it does
    Send 1 Spell or Trap from your Deck to the GY.
    Why you play it
    Send Disturbance, then banish it to add any Angelechy Spell or Trap (Problem, Opposition, e4). Or send Verdict, whose GY effect sends an Angelechy card from your hand or field to place a piece.
    Why 3
    It's one of your 13 starters. Only one per turn, so a second copy drawn the same turn becomes a spare for Problem. Opposition's GY effect shuffles a spent Disturbance or Verdict back, so later copies still have targets.
    Tips
    Ash can negate the send. Going 2nd it's the second engine card to go, after Terraforming: a plan that cuts two engine cards takes Terraforming and 1 FBG, one that cuts three takes Terraforming and 2 FBG. FBG through Disturbance usually becomes Opposition, which needs nothing else.

    What it does
    Gains effects from the Angelechy Monster Cards in your Spell & Trap Zone. 1+: draw 1 during their Standby Phase. 2+: you choose the zones they Special Summon to. 3+: once per turn, activate an Angelechy Trap the turn you Set it. 4+: once per turn, banish the monster(s) they Special Summon. 5: once per turn, Special Summon an Angelechy from your Extra Deck.

    Angelechy Opening to e4×3Your best card going 2nd: a board before they play

    Normal Trap · from your hand on their first Standby Phase

    What it does
    If your opponent goes first, you can activate it from your hand during the Standby Phase of their first turn. Place an Angelechy Field Spell from your hand, Deck or GY, Special Summon a Level 2 or 7 Angelechy (Enlisted, Castellan or Destrier) to the Extra Monster Zone, and place an Angelechy. Until the end of your next turn you can only Special Summon Synchros from the Extra Deck.
    Why you play it
    It builds before they act. Place Castellan and it starts the double e4: Castellan sends Verdict, Verdict places Bastion, Bastion places Shatranga, and Shatranga adds your 2nd e4, which isn't once per turn (line C, EG1). Going 1st, Set it as an interruption for their turn.
    Why 3
    39% to have it going 2nd. Never side it out going 2nd. One comes out going 1st for Boarder, because your e4 on their turn would put a Synchro on the field and give them a card type.
    Tips
    Use Fuwalos before e4; Fuwalos needs you to control no cards. Impulse negates e4. Ash on Castellan's send stops the loop; if you see Ash, place Bastion first and take a smaller board.
    Angelechy Verdict×2Negate at 1–4, banish their whole field at 5+

    Normal Trap · each effect once per turn

    What it does
    Counts the Angelechy Monster Cards on the whole field. 1–4: negate 1 face-up card they control. 5+: banish every card they control, without targeting. From the GY: banish it, send an Angelechy card from your hand or face-up field to the GY, then place an Angelechy with a name you don't already have placed.
    Why you play it
    It's your board's interruption and your way through theirs. The GY effect is a free placement: Castellan sends it, and you can chain it to put Bastion back when they Ghost Ogre or Typhoon your pieces (Lucas Sacco).
    Why 2
    Strategy Score and Shatranga add it, Castellan and Foolish Burial Goods send it, and Opposition shuffles a spent copy back. With 2, one can go to the GY for its placement while the other is still in the Deck to add and Set. In the mirror it's “the god card” (Trif): it counts both fields.
    Tips
    Count both fields: the Seneschal you gave them counts. It doesn't target, so Magician of Dark Chaos – Black Chaos ignores it (and stops their Spells and Traps from being banished), and Black Chaos ignores it while a Ritual Spell is in their GY.
    Angelechy Disturbance×1Mostly a GY search for any Angelechy Spell or Trap

    Normal Trap · each effect once per turn

    What it does
    Give your opponent control of an Angelechy monster you control until the End Phase, then negate their face-up cards in the zones next to it. From the GY: banish it to add any Angelechy Spell or Trap except Disturbance.
    Why you play it
    The GY effect is what Foolish Burial Goods and Castellan want: it becomes whichever piece you need. Handing them Enlisted also triggers Enlisted's return: it goes back to your Extra Deck and you Special Summon any Angelechy (line D).
    Why 1
    Opposition recycles it. Trif: you go through Disturbance and Verdict on turn 1, then shuffle them back. Lists run 1–3 (3 of the 8 pure Angelechy lists in the YCS Houston Top 32 ran 2). Never side out your only copy going 2nd.

    Extra Deck (15)

    Angelechy Enlisted×3The pawn: the body Problem and e4 summon

    Synchro Tuner · Level 2 · LIGHT Fairy · 1500/1500

    What it does
    Target an opponent's monster in the next column: banish it, then move Enlisted to their Main Monster Zone. When its control changes, it returns to the Extra Deck and its owner (you) Special Summons any Angelechy from the Extra Deck. Each effect once per turn.
    Why you play it
    It's the monster half of Problem and Opening to e4. On your turn it's removal that “promotes” into a bigger Angelechy. It's also your Tuner: with Destrier it makes Kusanagi (Level 9), with Bastion it makes Black-Winged Assault Dragon or Chaos Angel (Level 10), with Shatranga Centur-Ion Legatia (Level 12).
    Why 3
    The double e4 summons two and Problem a third. Verdict's GY effect sends one to the GY, and the others return to the Extra Deck when their control changes.
    Angelechy Castellan×1The bishop: sends Verdict or Disturbance, then locks chains

    Synchro · Level 7 · LIGHT Fairy · 2000/2500

    When placed
    Send 1 Angelechy card from your Deck to the GY, usually Verdict (for its placement) or Disturbance (for its search).
    While placed
    Each player can activate only 1 card or effect per Chain. They can't add a second hand trap to the same Chain, Elfnote can't chain Regina responses, and with Castellan placed, a Nibiru you activate in a Chain they started can't be answered: they've used their one activation in that Chain.
    As a monster
    Banish an opponent's monster in the next column, or a card in their Spell & Trap Zone 2 columns away.
    Why 1
    One per board is enough. The Louisville Top 4, Colin H. and 3 of the 8 YCS Houston Top 32 lists run 2 to cover it being used up or removed (if e4's Castellan gets Ashed or Ogred, you still want one for Opposition). Its send is the chokepoint of the double e4: Ash there stops the loop.

    1 · Introduction, theory and meta

    1.1 Why Angelechy, and why now

    Beyond the Brave (legal Oct 8) turned Angelechy from an Opening to e4 splash into a real deck. In the first weekend of regionals it won Belén, took 2nd at Burnaby and made six more Top 8s: second only to Elfnote Ars Magna. Jesse Kotton calls it the deck with “the best and most numerous hand trap lineup”, and DB Grinder names it one of the two real arguments against playing Elfnote. This guide explains why, card by card, and how to pilot it through the hand traps the whole format is siding against you.

    What makes it unfair

    1 · The engine dodges hand traps

    Your starters are Extra Deck summons and placements, not searches or monsters on the field. Veiler, Imperm and Nibiru have nothing to hit. Ash and Droll only hit the searches. Ghost Ogre destroys a placed piece, but its effect still resolves. Only Dominus Impulse stops Opposition, Problem and e4. The full table.

    2 · 23 interruptions and a board

    3 Ash, 2 Ghost Ogre, 3 Fuwalos, 2 Nibiru, 1 Droll, 9 Dominus Traps and 3 Opening to e4: 80% to open 3+ going second, 90% after siding. Most decks choose between hand traps and engine. Your Dominus are Set going first and used from the hand going second, and e4 is a hand trap that also builds your board during their Standby Phase.

    3 · Floodgates that sit in your backrow

    Shatranga: they can attempt only 5 monster effects a turn. Castellan: one card or effect per Chain. Destrier: 500 damage every time they activate anything. Bastion: your other Angelechy cards can't be destroyed. Endgame: you choose where they summon, and you banish their summon at 4 placed.

    4 · Two ways to break a board

    Verdict at 5+ Angelechy Monster Cards banishes everything they control, without targeting, on their turn. On yours, Kusanagi attacks every monster with 7,000 ATK and pierces, and Black-Winged Assault Dragon burns 700 per monster effect.

    Where it's weak

    • Dominus Impulse is the one hand trap that stops your starters. ROLAD, Mitsurugi, Maliss and the mirror main three. Bait it (3.5).
    • Ash or Ghost Ogre on Castellan's send stops the double e4 going second. Everyone sides 3 Ash, 3 Ghost Ogre and 3 Typhoon against you (DB Grinder's Houston advice).
    • Spell and Trap removal hits your placed pieces: Typhoon, Spell Shattering Sword, Hey, Space Trunade!, Feather Duster, The Fallen & The Virtuous. Bastion stops destruction only.
    • Black Chaos ignores your activated effects while a Ritual Spell is in their GY. It's what keeps ROLAD and Branded ROLAD close instead of favourable.
    • Jinzo – Energy Shocker turns off every Trap you play (e4, Verdict, all 9 Dominus), which makes Dark Time Wizard your one unfavoured matchup.
    • About 12% of hands have none of your 13 starters going first.
    The format, in one paragraph

    Since the October 19 list, Angelechy, Elfnote / Medius Ars Magna and Power Patron are the top three, so the mirror is the matchup you'll play most. Sky Striker, Maliss, Destiny HERO, Mermail and Invoked follow; the ROLAD pile, Branded and Yummy lost key cards. Angelechy is favoured against Elfnote (Shatranga caps their combo at 5 monster effects) and Power Patron; the mirror, Sky Striker, Maliss and Destiny HERO are close, and Dark Time Wizard is the one unfavoured matchup. 5.2 has the ranking and 1.4 Matchups at a glance every matchup.

    3 · Combos and how to beat hand traps

    3.2 Beating Ash Blossom and Dominus Purge

    Ash is in every opponent's deck and three more come in after siding. It negates an effect that adds a card from the Deck to the hand (draws count), Special Summons from the Deck, or sends from the Deck to the GY. Purge is a Trap that negates the adds. Both are once per turn, so the whole game is choosing what they hit.

    What they can hit

    • Strategy Score, Terraforming (adds)
    • Destrier's search when placed (add)
    • Shatranga's Trap add, from the Deck or the GY
    • Disturbance's GY search (add)
    • Castellan's send and Foolish Burial Goods (sends: Ash only, not Purge)
    • Endgame Problem's Standby draw (draws count as adds)

    What they can't

    • Opposition: an Extra Deck summon plus a placement
    • Problem: the discard summons from the Extra Deck and places
    • Opening to e4: placing a Field Spell from the Deck is neither an add nor a send
    • Verdict's GY placement and Bastion placing Shatranga
    • Enlisted, Seneschal, every banish on your pieces

    The rule: lead with what Ash can't touch, and have two searches

    Problem and Opposition make your first two pieces without giving Ash a target. Every board after that runs through one search (Destrier, Shatranga or Strategy Score). If your hand has two of those searches, one Ash can only stop one of them, and your board is the same either way. Count your searches before you start.

    1. Problem or Opposition first. They give Ash nothing, and every piece they place is already on the board when Ash comes.
    2. Hold Strategy Score as the back-up search. If they Ash Destrier or Shatranga, Strategy Score gets the same card. If they let both resolve, Strategy Score is still in your hand for next turn.
    3. Make them Ash the card you can replace. Players hold Ash for Strategy Score and for Castellan's send. If you have to give them a target, give them one you have a second route to.
    4. Foolish Burial Goods last, or as the Problem discard. Ash on it loses you the card and the Disturbance search behind it.
    5. Their Purge works on turn one. From the hand it only needs you to control a card, and you do as soon as you activate anything. Inspector Boarder doesn't stop it (it's a Trap); it only hits adds, so Foolish Burial Goods and Castellan's send are safe from it.

    Going second: Ash on Castellan's send

    The Standby double e4 has one choke point: Castellan sends Verdict from the Deck. Ash there stops the whole loop (DB Grinder). Your options:

    • Make them use Ash first. A Mulcharmy at their Standby (before your e4) is an Ash target too. If they Ash it, Castellan's send resolves; if they don't, your Mulcharmy taxes their whole turn.
    • Place Bastion instead of Castellan when you expect Ash and have no bait. Bastion places Shatranga (no Ash target), Shatranga adds your 2nd e4 (an Ash target, but then the 2nd e4 is a bonus, not the whole board).
    • Keep Problem as your field with the first e4 (place it from the Deck, keep any copy in your hand). If the loop stops, Problem is your turn.

    3.3 Beating Droll & Lock Bird

    Droll triggers after a card is added from the Main Deck to your hand outside the Draw Phase; draws count. That first card stays in your hand. For the rest of the turn, neither player can add from the Main Deck, and draws are stopped too. Elfnote, Power Patron and Blitzclique all main it.

    Stopped after Droll

    • Strategy Score, Terraforming
    • Destrier's search, Shatranga's add from the Deck, Disturbance's search
    • Triple Tactics Thrust's add mode
    • Any draw that turn, including Endgame's Standby draw

    Still works

    • Opposition, Problem, Opening to e4
    • Bastion → Shatranga, Verdict's GY placement
    • Castellan's send and Foolish Burial Goods (sends aren't adds)
    • Shatranga adding a Trap from the GY
    • Thrust's Set mode, Opposition's GY shuffle

    Three habits that beat it

    1. Make your first add the one that finishes the board. They'll Droll the first add, so make it the card that doesn't need another search: Destrier adding Opposition is the classic one.
    2. Put an Angelechy Trap in the GY early. Discard Verdict, Disturbance or an e4 to Problem, or send one with Castellan. Under Droll, Shatranga can still add it back from the GY.
    3. Lead with the cards that don't search. Problem and Opposition put pieces down without an add; save Strategy Score until you know whether they hold Droll.

    The Droll-proof Problem line

    1. Problem: discard an Angelechy Trap (e4, Verdict or Disturbance) → Special Summon Enlisted and place Destrier.
    2. Destrier adds Opposition. If they Droll now, Opposition is already in your hand.
    3. Opposition: Seneschal to their field, place Bastion → Shatranga. Shatranga adds from the GY the Trap you discarded (under Droll) or Verdict from the Deck (no Droll).
    4. Count: Enlisted + Destrier + Seneschal + Bastion + Shatranga = 5, plus Seneschal's free Fairy Synchro if you take it.

    Going second

    • On their turn, Droll can hit your Standby loop: if they Droll Shatranga's add, the rest of that turn has no adds and no draws, so Destrier's search and Endgame's Standby draw are gone too. Finish the loop's searches before you rely on Endgame.
    • Your Droll answers their Mulcharmy: after their first draw, Droll stops every draw that turn. It stops your own searches as well, so search first (3.4).

    3 · Combos and how to beat hand traps

    3.5 Beating Impulse, Ghost Ogre and backrow removal

    Dominus Impulse

    Impulse negates any effect that Special Summons: Opposition, Problem's discard, Opening to e4, Enlisted's return, Strategy Score's GY effect, Problem's revive, Endgame at 5. It destroys the card only if they have a Trap in their GY, which on turn one they usually don't, so a negated Problem stays on the field (its once per turn is used, but a second copy has its own).

    • Bait it with Problem before Opposition (DB Grinder). Opposition is the card you can't afford to lose; Problem is the one you can replace.
    • Impulse can't touch Verdict's GY placement, Bastion → Shatranga, Castellan's send or any search. If Opposition gets negated after Problem resolved, Verdict's GY placement still builds.
    • Count their Impulse: ROLAD, Mitsurugi, Maliss and Angelechy main three. Once one is used, it's once per turn.
    • From the hand it locks them out of LIGHT, EARTH and WIND monster effects for the rest of the duel; after that, their Ghost Ogre and Fuwalos are dead.

    Ghost Ogre & Snow Rabbit

    Ogre destroys a card that activates on the field: a placed piece's trigger, Problem, Enlisted. The effect still resolves.

    • Place Bastion first. While Bastion is placed, your other Angelechy cards can't be destroyed by their effects, so Ogre can only hit Bastion itself (when Bastion activates).
    • Problem's second effect: when a face-up Angelechy Monster Card you control is destroyed, return a placed Angelechy to the Extra Deck and Special Summon it. A destroyed piece becomes a monster that can banish.
    • Verdict in the GY: chain its placement to put Bastion (or a second Bastion) down in response (Lucas Sacco). With Castellan placed you only get one activation per Chain, so if you started that Chain, you can't add Verdict to it.

    Typhoon, Sword, Trunade and the rest

    • Destruction (Typhoon, Spell Shattering Sword, Fallen & Virtuous, Feather Duster, Lightning Storm): Bastion first; then only Bastion dies.
    • Bounce and banish (Hey, Space Trunade!, Black Chaos, Mirrorjade, Cosmic Cyclone) ignore Bastion. Opposition's GY effect shuffles banished pieces back so you can place them again.
    • Typhoon from their hand needs them to control no Spells/Traps and you to control 2+. Against you that's always true, so expect it.
    • Your Typhoon from the hand needs you to control no Spells/Traps: use it before you place anything.

    The cards built against you

    • Artifact Lancea: no banishing for the rest of the turn. Your Verdict and Disturbance GY effects (banish as a cost) and every banish stop. Placements and searches still work.
    • D.D. Crow: banishes Verdict from your GY before you use it. On your turn you get to act first after Castellan's send resolves, so use Verdict's GY effect right away and Crow is too late. On their turn (your Standby e4 loop) they act first and can banish it.
    • Skull Meister: negates an effect activated in your GY (Verdict, Disturbance, Opposition, Strategy Score).
    • Ghost Belle: only hits Opposition's GY shuffle (it returns cards from the GY to the Deck). Verdict's and Disturbance's GY effects banish as a cost, which Belle can't stop.

    4 · Rulings, tips and replays

    4.1 Rulings to know

    From the card texts. Points marked judge are readings to confirm with a judge before relying on them at an event.

    1. Castellan's lock is for both players. While it's placed, each player can activate only 1 card or effect per Chain, so you can't add a second effect to a Chain you started. If you activate Nibiru in a Chain they started (their summon's trigger), they've used their one activation and can't respond.
    2. Bastion protects every other Angelechy card on the field from destruction by their effects, including the Seneschal you gave them. Not itself, and not against banishing, bouncing or negating.
    3. A placed piece's “when placed” effect resolves even if Ghost Ogre destroys the piece in response.
    4. Verdict counts both fields and both zones. 1–4: negate one face-up card they control. 5+: banish everything they control, without targeting. Two exceptions from ROLAD: Magician of Dark Chaos – Black Chaos is unaffected by effects that don't target it, and it stops their Spells and Traps from being banished; Black Chaos is unaffected by all your activated effects while a Ritual Spell is in their GY. Answer Magician with a targeted banish (Enlisted, Shatranga, Castellan, Chaos Angel) and Black Chaos in battle (Kusanagi) or by removing the Ritual Spell from their GY.
    5. Shatranga caps attempts: their 6th monster effect can't even be activated, and negated activations still count.
    6. Draws count as adding from the Deck. Ash can negate draw effects (Mulcharmies, Endgame's draw), and Droll's lock stops draws for the rest of the turn.
    7. Placing a Field Spell from the Deck (Opening to e4) isn't an add or a send: Ash and Droll can't stop it.
    8. Opposition's restriction covers the whole turn you activate it: only LIGHT or DARK Synchros from the Extra Deck. Strategy Score's covers the rest of the turn after it resolves. e4's lasts until the end of your next turn: Synchros only (Kusanagi, Black-Winged Assault Dragon, Legatia and Chaos Angel are fine).
    9. Problem's once per turn belongs to each copy. A newly activated Problem gets its own use (line B). Its revive effect has no once per turn.
    10. Strategy Score's GY effect can't be used the turn it was sent there.
    11. Enlisted's return is activated by whoever controls it. Given to them, it's their monster effect: Destrier burns them and it counts toward Shatranga's cap. If they used Impulse from the hand (no LIGHT effects), it can't activate and stays on their field. judge whether it counts against Inspector Boarder's limit.
    12. Seneschal's summon trigger is mandatory. On their field it's their monster effect, which turns on Triple Tactics Thrust. judge
    13. Seneschal and the monsters its effect summons can't be Xyz or Link material and go back to the Extra Deck when they leave the field, so they can't be sent as a cost (Verdict).
    14. When an Angelechy targets Seneschal for an attack, the attack is negated and control passes to the other player: attack the one you gave them to take it back.
    15. Enlisted targeting Seneschal: Seneschal goes to the Extra Deck instead of being banished, and Enlisted doesn't move (line F). judge
    16. Endgame Problem: 2+ placed, you choose the zones their Special Summons go to; 3+, once per turn you can activate an Angelechy Trap the turn you Set it (line EG4) judge; 4+, once per turn banish what they Special Summon (not during the Damage Step).
    17. A new Field Spell sends the old one to the GY. e4 #2 placing Endgame sends Problem.
    18. Dominus from the hand locks Attributes for the rest of the duel; a Set Dominus locks nothing. Impulse and Purge destroy the card only if you have a Trap in your GY. Spark from the hand needs them to have activated a monster effect in the hand or GY that turn, and gives them a summon from the hand if you have no Trap in your GY.
    19. Triple Tactics Thrust's Set mode isn't an add, so it works under Droll. It needs their monster effect any time that turn, and Seneschal's trigger counts.
    20. Inspector Boarder counts monster card types on the field; placed pieces are Spells and don't count. Negated activations still count toward the limit.
    21. Kusanagi gains the original ATK of its Synchro materials: Enlisted (1,500) + Destrier (2,500) makes 7,000. Black-Winged Assault Dragon needs a Tuner Synchro (Enlisted) as material.
    4 · Rulings, tips and replays

    4.2 Tips worth remembering

    • Count both fields before Verdict. Their Seneschal and the Enlisted you passed count; anything they removed doesn't.
    • Monster hand traps first, Dominus from the hand last. Impulse from the hand turns off Ogre, Nibiru, Veiler, Fuwalos, Droll and your own Angelechy monster effects.
    • Fuwalos before e4. One card on your field and it's dead.
    • Bastion before the card you care about when they can destroy; Destrier first when you still need a Spell (DB Grinder).
    • Discard Angelechy Traps to Problem. Verdict, Disturbance or e4 in the GY are worth more than a Dominus you'll Set.
    • Keep your Problem in hand when e4 can place one from the Deck.
    • Problem first, Endgame second with one Endgame: if the loop stops, Problem is your turn.
    • Seneschal's zone matters. Against Elfnote, put it in their center zone (Regina summons only from there). For line F, put it next to the column your Enlisted will use. With Endgame at 2+, put their summons next to Castellan.
    • Skip Seneschal's free summon under Fuwalos or when you don't need the body.
    • Summon Synchros in Defense against ROLAD: Dark Charmer crashes into Attack Position.
    • Shuffle with Opposition's GY effect before your second search for a single copy: shuffle the spent Verdict back, then Disturbance or Shatranga finds it again.
    • Endgame's banish is once per turn: let a small summon through and banish the one that matters (TY-PHON, Junoldo's Synchro).
    • Opening to e4 only works from the hand during their first Standby Phase. Later, it's a Trap you Set.
    • Ash their Mulcharmy when it would cost you your turn; Droll it when your searches are done.
    • Read their cards. Opponents misread Angelechy and concede early; don't make the same mistake back (DB Grinder).

    The mistakes new players make most are in 1.3.

    Inspector Boarder, going first only

    Going 2nd: the Standby double e4

    1. Their Standby: Opening to e4 → summon Enlisted to the Extra Monster Zone, place Castellan, field = Problem.
    2. Castellan placed → send Verdict from the Deck.
    3. Verdict's GY effect (banish it + send the Enlisted) → place Bastion → Bastion places Shatranga.
    4. Shatranga adds your 2nd Opening to e4 → it isn't once per turn: activate it → summon again + place Destrier (search a Spell).

    Their whole turn is under Destrier burn, Shatranga's 5-effect cap, Castellan's one-per-chain and Bastion's protection. It beat Power Patron, Mitsurugi and ROLAD in the replays. Its chokepoint is Castellan's send (Ash, Ghost Ogre, Fallen & Virtuous). Pair it with a Mulcharmy so they spend Ash on that instead. With Endgame Problem: let the 2nd e4 place it (the 1st places Problem): draw 1, and at 4 placed it banishes what they Special Summon (Endgame lines).

    Rules and habits that came up

    • Opposition → place Destrier, not Bastion when you still want e4 and a Spell search later.
    • Triple Tactics Thrust: Set Opening to e4 when you've been hand-trapped (follow-up + interruption), not a Dominus; off the Opposition/Seneschal combo, add Opposition or Strategy Score.
    • Monster hand traps before Dominus from hand: Impulse locked a drawn Droll and cost a game vs Elfnote.
    • Enlisted given to them activates its return as their effect: it burns them (Destrier), counts for Shatranga's cap, and can't activate if they used Impulse from hand.
    • Seneschal and the monsters its effect summons return to the Extra Deck, so they can't be sent as Verdict/Brilliant Move costs and can't be Link/Xyz material.
    • Summon Synchros in Defense vs ROLAD (Dark Charmer crash). Kusanagi ends games; attacking Seneschal with an Angelechy takes it back.
    • Opponents concede early to this deck — and many misread it. Read their cards too (Grinder: “read the cards once a week”).
    The count that matters: Verdict looks at Angelechy monster cards on the whole field

    One or two copies are enough: Opposition's GY effect shuffles up to 5 spent Angelechy cards from your GY or banishment back into the Deck, including a Verdict or Disturbance whose GY effect you already used, so you can search the same copy again the same turn.

    Both sides count, and so do cards in the Spell & Trap Zone. A Seneschal you put on their field with Opposition counts toward your 5. At 5+, Verdict banishes every card they control, without targeting.

    It's the Field Spell Opening to e4 places from the Deck.     EG3 · Going 1st: Set e4, Endgame at their Standby (game 1)

    DB Grinder replays

    You end your turn with Problem as the field and e4 Set; at their Standby, e4 swaps the field to Endgame. Your going-1st siding takes Endgame out, so this is for game 1 (after siding, e4 places a Problem instead).

    1. Your turn (Grinder's correction on the Power Patron replay): Opposition → Seneschal to their field and place Destrier, not Bastion → Destrier adds Problem → Problem: discard → Enlisted + a placement. Set e4 and Verdict.
    2. Their Standby: flip e4 → place Endgame (Problem goes to the GY), summon Enlisted, place Bastion → Bastion places Shatranga → Shatranga adds Verdict.
    3. Endgame: draw 1.

    Grinder: “4 placed + Verdict set, field draw, more options”, and with Endgame at 4+ Junoldo's Synchro is banished on summon. e4 needs a free Spell & Trap Zone for its placement (it's still on the field while it resolves) and an open Extra Monster Zone. With Boarder out, the Enlisted it summons gives the field a card type — one more monster effect for each player.

    EG4 · Your turn after line EG1: Verdict the turn you Set it

    From the card texts — not seen in a replay yet

    Endgame at 3+ lets you activate an Angelechy Trap the turn you Set it, and Verdict with 5+ Angelechy Monster Cards on the field banishes everything they control.

    1. After line EG1 you have 4 placed + Enlisted in the Extra Monster Zone = 5. Keep Endgame as your field — don't activate Problem yet.
    2. Strategy Score (Destrier's search) → add Verdict.
    3. Set Verdict and activate it now (Endgame's 3+ effect): banish all cards your opponent controls.
    4. Then Problem from your hand if you need more (it replaces Endgame), and attack.

    Needs a Verdict in the Deck: you run 2, and line EG1 banishes one — Opposition's GY effect can shuffle it back first. Count again before you commit: anything they removed lowers it, and at 1–4 Verdict only negates one card. Bastion keeps Endgame from being destroyed, not from Hey, Space Trunade! or a banish. Check the timing with a judge before relying on it.

    Playing Endgame well
    • One copy: Problem with the 1st e4, Endgame with the 2nd. Two copies: Endgame both times, drawing each time.
    • 2+ placed: you choose their summon zones — put their monsters in a column next to Castellan, whose banish targets a monster in its adjacent column. Against Elfnote, put Regina outside the center Main Monster Zone: her Deck summon only works from the center (Lucas Sacco).
    • 4+ is optional and once per turn: let a small summon through and banish the one that matters (TY-PHON, Junoldo's Synchro, their boss).
    • 5 placed: a free Angelechy from your Extra Deck every turn, but your Spell & Trap Zone is full, so no Set traps.
    • What beats it: Ghost Ogre or Ash on Castellan's send (the loop stops), Hey, Space Trunade! bouncing placed cards (Elfnote sides 2; a Mermail guide recommends it against you), Typhoon on Bastion first.

    Chaospawn Bishop + Witness of the Ancient

    Line: Normal Summon Bishop → its effect summons a 2nd Bishop from the Deck (it becomes LIGHT) → Level 8 Synchro Glitch Clutch Nullgainer → Nullgainer searches Witness of the Ancient (Level 8, 0 ATK) → Witness Special Summons itself → it places up to 3 Synchros (Bastion, Destrier, Castellan) and makes an Arc Token.     Strengths

    More starters, and a fast route to 5 Angelechys for Verdict's banish-all. Witness can place from the GY as well, so Synchros that were destroyed come back as placements. It doesn't need Problem, so it plays through cards that answer Spell starters.

    Weaknesses

    FTK

    Combo (from the Centur-Ion FTK list on YGOPRODeck and Duel Evolution's FTK video):

    1. Bishop → Nullgainer → Witness, which places Bastion and Destrier.
    2. Destrier adds Opposition. Bastion places Shatranga, which adds Disturbance.
    3. Witness + Nullgainer → Ultimaya Tzolkin. Setting Opposition triggers Tzolkin, which summons Beelze of the Diabolic Dragons.
    4. Opposition puts Seneschal on their field and places Castellan. Seneschal lets you summon Cupid Pitch, and Castellan sends Brilliant Move to the GY.
    5. Cupid Pitch + the token → Mind Castlin. Brilliant Move from the GY → Enlisted. Enlisted + Mind Castlin → Scarlight Red Dragon Archfiend.
    6. Mind Castlin swaps Beelze onto their field. Scarlight destroys and burns.
    7. Their Beelze gains ATK each time they take damage. That's an effect they activate, so Destrier (placed) burns 500, which triggers Beelze again, and it loops until they're at 0.

    Centur-Ion starters (Primera, Trudea, Stand Up) or Ascator can stand in for Bishop, since the combo only needs to reach Nullgainer.

    Strengths

    A turn-1 win from one card if nothing interrupts it. If the line is stopped after Witness, you still have a placed Angelechy board.

    Weaknesses

    Ash, Veiler, Ogre, Droll, Imperm, Nibiru or Harmonia each stop it. Duel Evolution's list mains only 3 Ash for interaction, so going 2nd is very weak. It needs 7–8 Extra Deck slots outside the Angelechy engine. And Beelze, Mind Castlin and Tzolkin are obvious ban targets: Flipside's July 2026 Deck Debut names them as the cards to hit.

    Siding planner

    Pick a deck and who goes first. The swaps come straight from the table below.

    Trif's rules (Oct 11 video), applied to you

    • Know the format first. Medius variants and the 60-card ROLAD pile are the decks that matter; Angelechy needs its own plan.
    • Going 2nd, go big: 24+ hand traps after siding, cutting up to ~8 engine cards. Mulcharmys count as half a play.
    • Think in pairs: Veiler + Nibiru, Veiler + Droll, Ash + Nibiru. Don't Veiler their first play: let it search, then Droll.
    • Pick your best 8 of ~10 side hand traps per matchup instead of fixed swaps.
    • Going 1st, add plays, not more negation. For you that's Boarder + Thrust; your Set Dominus traps are the negation.

    Your templates

    Going 1st: −3 Fuwalos (dead), −1 Endgame Problem (a going-2nd card), −1 Opening to e4 (with Boarder out, your own e4 on their turn summons a Synchro and gives them a card type), −1 Nibiru (Boarder keeps most decks under 5 summons) → +3 Boarder, +3 Thrust (their monster hand trap turns it on, and its Set mode works under Droll). Against Elfnote and pure Branded the Droll comes out instead of the Nibiru: they play through a lone Droll. Against Mitsurugi, Destiny HERO and Invoked both Ghost Ogre come out instead of the e4 and the Nibiru (all 3 e4 stay, since Boarder is weakest there anyway). Sky Striker: both Nibiru come out instead of the e4 (few summons). The mirror: no Boarder — Fuwalos, Droll and both Nibiru out for the 3rd Ghost Ogre, 2 Typhoon and 3 Thrust. Going 2nd in the mirror, the 3rd Ogre, 2 Typhoon and 3 Thrust come in for Droll, both Nibiru and all 3 Spark (Opposition's Seneschal turns Thrust on).

    Going 2nd (only cards that matter): your main already has 23 interruptions (80% to open 3+; Trif's minimum is 24). Bring only the side cards that matter in the matchup — 2 Veiler (engines that run on the field), the 3rd Ghost Ogre (Elfnote, Yummy, Maliss, the mirror), 2 Typhoon (face-up Spells/Traps that matter), Lava Golem (bosses your effects can't touch: Black Chaos, Mirrorjade, Liger Dancer, and big monster boards) — making room from the engine in this order: Terraforming (it only finds Problem), then Foolish Burial Goods (Opposition shuffles its targets back, so later copies still work), then the 2nd FBG. Most plans end at 24–26 (84–90% to open 3+); Lava Golem breaks a board rather than interrupting, so plans that bring it count lower. Never cut: Opposition (your best card), Verdict, Disturbance and Endgame (the engine fetches them) or e4. Main cards that are bad in the matchup come out first: Ghost Ogre (Mitsurugi, Destiny HERO, Invoked), Droll (mirror, pure Branded), Nibiru (mirror, Sky Striker), Fuwalos (Blitzclique), Spark (all 3 against Dark Time Wizard, Sky Striker and the mirror).

    Order on their turn: Mulcharmy first (Fuwalos or Purulia, before e4 at their Standby), then Ash / Droll, then Spark or Purge from hand (they leave Ghost Ogre, Veiler and Nibiru usable), then Ogre / Veiler / Nibiru, and Impulse last (it turns off your LIGHT monsters and your Angelechy monster-zone effects).

    Situational side-ins: Brilliant Move

    No standard plan includes it. Brilliant Move returns a placed Angelechy to the Extra Deck and Special Summons it; from the GY, banish it and send an Angelechy card from your hand or face-up field to Special Summon any Angelechy from your Extra Deck. It needs a piece already placed, so it's a card for a turn where your engine has already worked: usually your first turn going second, after a Standby Opening to e4.

    Bring it in when

    • You have to win in battle, going second. Black Chaos (ROLAD pile and Branded ROLAD, while a Ritual Spell is in their GY), Lunalight Liger Dancer and Red-Eyes Black Dragon Exceed (Dark Time Wizard) ignore your effects, but they lose to Kusanagi. After a Standby e4 you have Enlisted in the Extra Monster Zone and a placed Destrier: Brilliant Move returns Destrier and summons it, and Enlisted + Destrier makes Kusanagi (7,000 ATK, attacks every monster, piercing). Swap: −1 Strategy Score +1 Brilliant Move (Strategy Score is next in the going-2nd engine cut order); against Dark Time Wizard, −1 Dominus Purge instead, since Jinzo turns your Traps off.
    • You've seen them win long games against you. Discarded to Problem, it still works from the GY that turn or any later one: a free Angelechy summon, such as Castellan to banish, or a body for Black-Winged Assault Dragon or Chaos Angel. Swap it for a Strategy Score.

    Leave it out

    • Going first. On turn one it only works after your engine already has, so it's a win-more card (Trif: “win more… don't play it”).
    • When they still hold Impulse. Both effects Special Summon, so Impulse negates them. Against ROLAD, Mitsurugi and Maliss (3 Impulse each), use it after they've spent one.

    When they hit you

    • Ash on a search (Strategy Score, Destrier, Shatranga, Disturbance): it's once per turn, so lead with the search you can afford to lose; Problem's summon and every placement can't be Ashed.
    • Impulse on Problem or Opposition: on turn 1 their GY has no Trap, so the card isn't destroyed. Switch to your other summon route; Bastion → Shatranga and Verdict's GY placement can't be Impulsed.
    • Droll: stop searching from the Deck. Shatranga can still add a Trap from the GY (discard Verdict to Problem first).
    • Ghost Ogre: destroys the card, but its effect still resolves. With Problem on the field, a destroyed Angelechy comes back as a summon.
    • Typhoon from hand: place Bastion before anything else you care about; it protects the others but not itself.

          How it counts
          • Jobs. Each main-deck card has a job you can change: Starter (makes a play by itself), Starter + a spare (needs another Spell or Trap to discard, like Angelechy Problem), Extender (continues a play), Interruption, Going-1st back-up (a second route with a starter, like Thrust or Boarder) and Brick.           • Survives one hand trap: two plays, or a play plus an extender or a going-1st back-up.
