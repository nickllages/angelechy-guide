THE GOING-2ND e4 LOOP at their Standby Phase: e4 -> summon to EMZ + place Castellan -> Castellan placed: send Verdict from Deck -> Verdict GY (banish + send an Angelechy) places Bastion -> Bastion places Shatranga -> Shatranga adds the 2nd Opening to e4 (from Deck/GY) -> e4 is NOT once per turn, activate it again from hand -> Enlisted + Endgame Problem + another placement -> Endgame draw at Standby. Grinder counted "+7" before PP did anything. Board on their turn: Destrier burn 500/activation, Shatranga 5-monster-effect cap, Castellan 1 card/effect per chain, Endgame zone choice.
  PP: Scale pop -> Terminus; Medius; Angelechy Impulsed the Theorealize Medius summon (destroyed it; Impulse needs a Trap in GY to destroy — Verdict was there). Castellan's one-per-chain stopped PP from using both banished Ars Magna effects in one chain. PP passed weak; Angelechy: Strategy Score -> Opposition (Seneschal to them: mandatory trigger lets both players summon a negated Fairy Synchro; Seneschal + Destrier burn) -> Brilliant Move; PP scooped at 500 LP.
- G2 Angelechy 2nd again with Problem, Opposition, Purge, Dominus Spark, e4; PP had Ars Magica spell, Medius, Terminus, Fuwalos, Infinity. PP chained FUWALOS to Angelechy's Standby e4 (they control no cards at that point) -> drew off every Extra Deck summon. Angelechy stopped early (Enlisted + Disturbance search + Endgame draw). (2) Expect Fuwalos chained to e4 at Standby — your summons draw them cards; consider stopping at a sufficient board. (3) Castellan's one-per-chain lock is excellent vs Ars Magna (they want to chain banished effects). (4) Their outs to our board are negation (Junaldo's Synchro negate-all, Theorealize Medius), not destruction.

## DB Grinder — "Cards to side vs Angelechy at YCS Houston" (EhiqvXDuxLM, Oct 6) — what opponents will bring
- S:P Little Knight (banish) — but with Castellan placed they get one effect per chain, so they can't chain around our response.
- Power Patron Link-3 that banishes the board; Junaldo -> Synchro negate-all (Grinder: "not consistent" — Endgame at 4+ banishes it on summon; Spark, Ogre, Impulse, Songs also stop it).
- Verdict in the mirror: counts Angelechy monster cards on BOTH fields, so it banishes the opponent's whole field consistently; also negates board breakers (Evenly Matched etc.).
- Gallant Bandit: when we leave Seneschal/Enlisted on their field or end on one Synchro + Standby e4, Bandit tributes both monsters and they just attack. Hey, Space Trunade!: "okay" going 1st, Verdict negates it.
- Mass banish Links: Topologic Zeroboros (banish all cards when something is SS'd to its zone) and a similar banish-all Link — banish ignores Bastion.
- ASH: Strategy Score; whatever Problem/Opposition/Verdict places; Disturbance's search; and above all CASTELLAN's Deck send during the e4 loop — "if you just Ash the Castellan, e4 only sets up a Castellan".
- GHOST OGRE "excellent": pops Problem or any placed card, including Bastion itself (it only protects the OTHER Angelechy cards). Our counter: if they Ogre Problem after we discarded Verdict, chain Verdict's GY effect to send Problem and place another.
- Dominus Impulse "stops everything" (e4, Opposition, Problem). Purge "good". Fallen & Virtuous pops Castellan in response to e4. PSY-Framegear Epsilon/Gamma (negate e4/Dominus) as tech.
- TYPHOON: "side three, 100%". Griffoh -> Spell Shattering Sword (destroy all our face-up Spells) — Bastion blanks it except for Bastion. Cosmic Cyclone going 1st.
- His rule: "3 Ash, 3 Ghost Ogre, 3 Typhoon mandatory somewhere in your 55 for Houston." People stopped dropping Fuwalos at Standby (so e4 Standby lines face fewer Fuwalos).
