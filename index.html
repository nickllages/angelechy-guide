The lock only applies when you activate the trap from your hand; a Set Dominus has none. Your Angelechys are all LIGHT except Seneschal, which is DARK.

Rule of thumb: the lock only stops cards you haven't used yet, so it's a sequencing problem, not a reason to side the Dominus out. Going 2nd, drop Fuwalos, Ash, Droll, Ghost Ogre or Nibiru first, then the Dominus from hand. When you need the Angelechy monster-zone banishes later, use Purge from hand before Impulse. Going 1st, Set them (Triple Tactics Thrust can Set them from the Deck) and there's no lock at all.

Inspector Boarder, going first only

Boarder lets each player activate only as many monster effects per turn as there are monster card types on the field (Ritual, Fusion, Synchro, Xyz, Pendulum, Link). Normal Summon it before anything else. Your Angelechys in the Spell & Trap Zone are Continuous Spells, so they don't add a type, and their “placed” effects are Spell effects that Boarder ignores. Pendulum Scales are Spell Cards too, so I'm only counting monsters in Monster Zones; confirm both points with a judge.

On your turn

While no Extra Deck monster is in a Monster Zone the count is 0, so they can't activate Ash, Droll, Ghost Ogre, Nibiru, Veiler, Fuwalos or Purulia at all. Their Trap hand traps (Imperm, Dominus, Opening to e4) still work. Each Enlisted or Seneschal you put out raises the count to 1, which gives them one monster effect, so do your searches while the count is still 0.

On their turn

With 0 types on the field, their first monster effect has to wait until they put an Extra Deck or Ritual monster out without using one. Each extra type they want costs another summon that also avoids monster effects. Your own monster hand traps get the same cap, so lean on your Set Traps (6 Dominus, Verdict).

Angelechy lines through multiple hand traps

Most of the engine doesn't care about common hand traps. Only searches can be hit by Ash, Droll and Purge. Summon effects can be hit by Impulse. Ghost Ogre destroys the card but the effect still resolves. Everything that only places an Angelechy, or that works from the GY, ignores all of them.

What the replays taught (DB Grinder, 8 Angelechy games with the new support)

Going 2nd: the Standby double e4

  1. Their Standby: Opening to e4 → summon Enlisted to the Extra Monster Zone, place Castellan, field = Problem.
  2. Castellan placed → send Verdict from the Deck.
  3. Verdict's GY effect (banish it + send the Enlisted) → place Bastion → Bastion places Shatranga.
  4. Shatranga adds your 2nd Opening to e4 → it isn't once per turn: activate it → summon again + place Destrier (search a Spell).

Their whole turn is under Destrier burn, Shatranga's 5-effect cap, Castellan's one-per-chain and Bastion's protection. It beat Power Patron, Mitsurugi and ROLAD in the replays. Its chokepoint is Castellan's send (Ash, Ghost Ogre, Fallen & Virtuous). Pair it with a Mulcharmy so they spend Ash on that instead. With Endgame Problem: let the 2nd e4 place it (the 1st places Problem): draw 1, and at 4 placed it banishes what they Special Summon (why one Endgame).

Rules and habits that came up

  • Opposition → place Destrier, not Bastion when you still want e4 and a Spell search later.
  • Triple Tactics Thrust: Set Opening to e4 when you've been hand-trapped (follow-up + interruption), not a Dominus; or add Talent off the Opposition/Seneschal combo (Tyler Holland).
  • Monster hand traps before Dominus from hand: Impulse locked a drawn Droll and cost a game vs Elfnote.
  • Enlisted given to them activates its return as their effect: it burns them (Destrier), counts for Shatranga's cap, and can't activate if they used Impulse from hand.
  • Seneschal and the monsters its effect summons return to the Extra Deck, so they can't be sent as Verdict/Brilliant Move costs and can't be Link/Xyz material.
  • Summon Synchros in Defense vs ROLAD (Dark Charmer crash). Kusanagi ends games; attacking Seneschal with an Angelechy takes it back.
  • Opponents concede early to this deck — and many misread it. Read their cards too (Grinder: “read the cards once a week”).
The count that matters: Verdict looks at Angelechy monster cards on the whole field

Single copies are enough: Opposition's GY effect shuffles up to 5 spent Angelechy cards from your GY or banishment back into the Deck, including a Verdict or Disturbance whose GY effect you already used, so you can search the same copy again the same turn.

Both sides count, and so do cards in the Spell & Trap Zone. A Seneschal you put on their field with Opposition counts toward your 5. At 5+, Verdict banishes every card they control, without targeting.

A · Opposition first, Seneschal across

Not in guides I reviewed

Needs Opposition plus access to Problem (Problem, Terraforming, or Strategy Score) and one spare Spell/Trap to discard.

  1. Opposition: Special Summon Seneschal to their Main Monster Zone and place Bastion. Your Bastion trigger and their mandatory Seneschal trigger chain together. Seneschal's effect lets you Special Summon a Fairy Synchro too: take Destrier #2 (negated). Its body counts for Verdict and is Kusanagi material later.
  2. Bastion places Shatranga. Shatranga adds Verdict from the Deck. This is the line's only search that matters.
  3. Problem: discard a spare Spell/Trap (ideally Brilliant Move or Disturbance, for their GY effects) → Special Summon Enlisted and place Destrier. Destrier adds Strategy Score, which adds Opening to e4 or a second Verdict.
  4. Set Verdict. The count is now Seneschal + Destrier #2 + Enlisted + Bastion + Shatranga + Destrier = 6. On their turn, after they commit, Verdict banishes everything they control.

Gets through: Ash (any one search), Droll (once Verdict is in hand nothing else needs the Deck), Veiler, Imperm, Nibiru, and Ghost Ogre on Bastion (Shatranga still lands; Bastion protects every other Angelechy on the field from destruction, including their Seneschal). Loses to: Impulse on Opposition plus a second answer to Problem. Against their Fuwalos: skip Destrier #2 to give one fewer draw.

Bonus: Seneschal's trigger is a monster effect they activated, and they now control a monster. That turns on Triple Tactics Thrust (sided in going 1st) in “add” mode, so it searches any Normal Spell/Trap (Strategy Score, Opening to e4, Verdict, a Dominus). It also turns on your sided Triple Tactics Talent (the effect was activated during your Main Phase): draw 2, or look at their hand and shuffle away their best card. Seneschal also can't be used for Xyz or Link Summons. Check the activation-control reading with a judge before relying on it in a sanctioned match.

With Boarder out: Seneschal's 3,000 ATK can attack over Boarder (2,000) with no effect needed. With Boarder out, use line F: Enlisted sends Seneschal back to your Extra Deck the same turn. Talent's hand look can shuffle away the Imperm they'd use on Boarder.

B · Two Problems on one turn

Not in guides I reviewed

Needs Problem or Terraforming plus two spare Spells/Traps. Problem's “once per turn” belongs to that copy, so a newly activated Problem gets its own use.

  1. Problem #1: discard → Special Summon Enlisted, place Destrier. Destrier adds Problem #2.
  2. Activate Problem #2 (#1 goes to the GY). Discard → Enlisted #2, place Bastion → Shatranga → add Verdict.
  3. Count: Enlisted ×2 + Destrier + Bastion + Shatranga = 5. Set Verdict, plus Opening to e4 or a Dominus.

Gets through: Droll after Destrier resolves (if you discarded a Verdict, Shatranga can add it back from the GY), Veiler, Imperm, Nibiru, Ghost Ogre. Loses to: Ash on Destrier if you don't also hold a second Problem.

C · Double e4 on their standby (going 2nd)

Known line, tuned for your 40
  1. Fuwalos first, while you control no cards, then Opening to e4: place Problem from the Deck, Special Summon Enlisted to the Extra Monster Zone, place Castellan → send Verdict to the GY.
  2. Verdict (GY): send Enlisted, which frees the Extra Monster Zone → place Bastion → Shatranga → add Opening to e4 #2.
  3. e4 #2: Special Summon Enlisted again and place Destrier → add Opposition for your turn. Their turn now runs under a 5-monster-effect cap, one card per chain, and 500 burn per activation.

Ash on Castellan's send stops the whole loop (DB Grinder). If you see Ash, place Bastion first instead and accept a smaller board. Imperm Set in an empty column can lock your last open zone. Answer it by having Destrier add Brilliant Move and activating it in that column first: if it's negated, banish it from the GY to Special Summon and free a zone (7-0 profile).

D · Breaking a board on your turn (going 2nd)

Known finisher
  • Assault Blackwing – Kusanagi: Enlisted (Lv2 Tuner) + a Lv7 Destrier, either from Seneschal's effect or Brilliant Move's GY effect, makes Lv9. It gains its materials' original ATK (~7,000 with Destrier), attacks every monster and pierces. Opposition's lock still allows it because it's DARK.
  • Chaos Angel: Enlisted + Bastion (Lv8) → banish 1 card when summoned; with LIGHT materials your Synchros are unaffected by their monster effects.
  • Disturbance Set on your turn and used on theirs: give Enlisted to them → negate their adjacent face-up cards. Enlisted's control change returns it to your Extra Deck and you Special Summon any Angelechy, such as Castellan to banish.

E · Boarder first (going 1st)

Built around your side deck

Needs Inspector Boarder + Strategy Score + access to Problem. Uses the window where they can't activate monster effects at all.

  1. Normal Summon Boarder. There are no Extra Deck types on the field, so no monster hand trap can be activated against anything that follows.
  2. Strategy Score → add Verdict. Ash and Droll can't be used here; only Purge (a Trap) can stop it.
  3. Problem: discard Verdict → Enlisted (the count is now 1, so they get one monster effect this turn) + place Bastion → Shatranga → add Disturbance (or Opening to e4). If they spend their one effect here, Bastion and Shatranga still land.
  4. Verdict (GY): send Enlisted, which puts the count back to 0 → place Destrier → add Brilliant Move or a second Problem.
  5. Set Impulse or Purge (or Disturbance, if you kept an Enlisted out).

End: Boarder, Problem, Bastion, Shatranga and Destrier, two Set Traps, and 0 monster types. On their turn they get no monster effects until they summon an Extra Deck or Ritual monster without one; even then they're under Shatranga's 5-effect cap and Destrier's burn. Gets through: every monster hand trap until step 3, then one of them; Ghost Ogre on a placed card doesn't stop its effect. Loses to: Imperm from hand on Boarder (it's off until the end of the turn), Purge on Strategy Score, and Impulse on Problem.

F · Boarder + Seneschal (your line)

Not in guides I reviewed

Needs Inspector Boarder + Opposition + access to Problem. Their monster hand traps stay locked for your whole turn, and both Triple Tactics cards turn on.

  1. Normal Summon Boarder. There are 0 types on the field, so nothing they have that's a monster can respond.
  2. Opposition: Special Summon Seneschal to their Main Monster Zone, in a column next to where you'll put Enlisted, and place Bastion. Seneschal is a Synchro, so the count becomes 1, and its mandatory trigger is their one allowed monster effect for the turn: their hand traps stay locked for the rest of your turn. (Skip Seneschal's free summon for you; it adds nothing here.) Bastion places Shatranga, which adds Verdict.
  3. Triple Tactics Thrust / Talent are now live (they activated a monster effect during your Main Phase and control a monster). Thrust adds any Normal Spell/Trap from your Deck; Talent draws 2 or shuffles away the Imperm they'd use on Boarder.
  4. Problem: discard Verdict → Enlisted in a column next to Seneschal's, and place Destrier → add Strategy Score (you need an Angelechy card in hand for step 6).
  5. Enlisted targets Seneschal (your one monster effect for the turn). Seneschal's own text sends it back to your Extra Deck instead of being banished, so the banish never happens and Enlisted's “then move this card” doesn't either. Enlisted stays on your field and their field is empty.
  6. Verdict (GY): banish it, send an Angelechy card from your hand (the Strategy Score) → place Castellan → send Disturbance from the Deck to the GY.
  7. Opposition (GY): banish it → shuffle the banished Verdict (plus anything else spent) back into the Deck. Then Disturbance (GY): banish it → add that Verdict again.
  8. Choose your finish. (a) Wipe: Set Verdict. Count: Bastion, Shatranga, Destrier, Castellan + Enlisted = 5, so it banishes everything they control on their turn. Enlisted keeps them at 1 type, so they get one monster effect. (b) Full lock: in step 6, send Enlisted instead of the hand card. That's 0 types on their turn, so no monster effects until they summon around Boarder. The count drops to 4, so the Set Verdict negates 1 card instead.

Gets through: every monster hand trap. Loses to: Impulse on Opposition (no Seneschal, so use line E), Purge on Shatranga's search, Imperm from hand on Boarder. This line needs your one Disturbance in the Deck for step 6 (the old “Set your second Disturbance” trick needed 2 copies). Confirm all three Boarder interactions with a judge: Seneschal's trigger counting as theirs, Enlisted not moving, and Enlisted's mandatory effect counting against their cap.

Why one Angelechy Endgame Problem (since Oct 12, in the 2nd Disturbance's slot)

It's the Field Spell Opening to e4 places from the Deck. Its effects grow with the Angelechy Monster Cards in your Spell & Trap Zone: 1+ draw 1 during their Standby Phase · 2+ you choose the zones they Special Summon to · 3+ once per turn, activate an Angelechy Trap the turn you Set it · 4+ once per turn, banish the monster(s) they Special Summon · 5 once per turn, Special Summon an Angelechy from your Extra Deck.

Going 2nd: e4 from hand at their Standby → Endgame from the Deck → draw 1 right away (it's still their Standby). In the double e4, let the 1st e4 place Problem and the 2nd place Endgame: with Castellan, Bastion, Shatranga and Destrier placed it's at 4, so it also banishes what they Special Summon. Going 1st: a Set e4 at their Standby does the same, but it sends your Problem to the GY (no more float on a destroyed Angelechy), so do it when the draw or the banish is worth more. Drawn: a Spell for Problem's discard. Lists run 1–2 (Team Forbidden: “only need one — you place it off e4 on their turn”).

Keys to the matchup

  1. Fuwalos, Nibiru and Ghost Ogre were the Louisville Top 4's MVPs here; a single Droll or Ash gets played through.
  2. Sequence: monster hand traps first, Dominus from hand last (Impulse from hand locks Fuwalos, Veiler, Ogre and Nibiru).

The Elfnote singers (Lucina, Tinia, Fortuna) Special Summon themselves; Elfnote Power Patron raises a Level and Synchro Summons on the spot; the Medius / Power Patron package and the new Ars Magna Level 8s (banish from hand to search, return from banishment when a Link or matching type is summoned) tie it together. What they do to you

Their answers to your board are negation and banishing (Crystal Wing, Number 90, Mediclius, Theorealized Medius) plus hand rips (Gottoms, Tinia) that take e4 or your hand traps, so don't sandbag. Seneschal on their field clogs a zone they can't Link or Xyz off. They side: Ghost Ogre, Typhoon, Hey, Space Trunade! (bounces placed cards; DB Grinder: “definitely side into this matchup”), Feather Duster, Lightning Storm, Evenly Matched.

Boarder is at its best: their line is roughly 25 monster effects, and their only ways around it without spending an effect are summon procedures (a normal Synchro or Link). Set Impulse stops Past Lull and Welcome Home; Purge stops Terminus and the Ars Magna searches; Verdict negates what's left. Castellan placed stops them chaining Regina responses. Attack over Boarder isn't a worry: “if you have Boarder you've normally already won”.

His reasons, from the profile

2 Endgame Problem: an e4 target going 2nd, one draw per copy. 2 Verdict, 1 Disturbance: “Disturbance is the better Foolish” target for Castellan to send, but he wants a second Verdict so one isn't stranded. Legatia: Enlisted on a Level 10 makes Level 12, which draws and removes.

Cut the Chaospawn Bishops (he ran 2, and the FTK package before that): Bishop “will lose to cards you shouldn't be losing to”, such as Veiler, Harmonia and Imperm.

Siding. Going 2nd, he sides out the Dominus cards that clash with monster hand traps and brings in Purulia, because “you either choose one or the other”. Going 1st, he sides out Fuwalos for draw and going-first cards (Traptantalizing Tune discards a Normal Trap to draw 2, and this build is mostly Normal Traps), sometimes plus the 2nd Thrust. Lancea is for ROLAD, which clears Angelechy boards by banishing. Hey, Space Trunade! bounces cards instead of destroying them, so it gets past Bastion's protection.

Strengths

Twelve hand-activated traps let you interrupt their first turn without monster hand traps. Songs and Spark don't touch your own Angelechys: the Synchros come from the Extra Deck, the placed cards are Spells, and the monsters are LIGHT/DARK, which Spark allows. That means fewer dead draws against hand-trap-light decks. Going 1st, you have a board plus traps you can fire from hand, and you don't depend on Boarder.

Weaknesses

Songs can only be activated from hand with no monsters in your GY, so one Ash or Fuwalos turns it off. Once used, it stops your hand traps until the end of the next turn. Spark (EARTH/WATER/FIRE/WIND) plus Purge (DARK/WATER/FIRE) leaves only LIGHT monster effects, and Impulse takes LIGHT too. You're down to 6 monster hand traps, so against Elfnote Ars Magna going 2nd you have fewer outs. Spark lets them summon from hand if you have no Trap in the GY.

Chaospawn Bishop + Witness of the Ancient

Line: Normal Summon Bishop → its effect summons a 2nd Bishop from the Deck (it becomes LIGHT) → Level 8 Synchro Glitch Clutch Nullgainer → Nullgainer searches Witness of the Ancient (Level 8, 0 ATK) → Witness Special Summons itself → it places up to 3 Synchros (Bastion, Destrier, Castellan) and makes an Arc Token. Bastion then places Shatranga, so one card puts 4 Angelechys in the Spell & Trap Zone. Both locks (Bishop: LIGHT/DARK and Synchro only; Opposition and Strategy Score: LIGHT/DARK Synchros only) fit Angelechy.

Strengths

More starters, and a fast route to 5 Angelechys for Verdict's banish-all. Witness can place from the GY as well, so Synchros that were destroyed come back as placements. It doesn't need Problem, so it plays through cards that answer Spell starters.

Weaknesses

Every step is a monster effect: Veiler/Ogre on Bishop, Ash on the Deck summon or Nullgainer's search, Droll, Imperm, Fydraulis Harmonia. It also uses your Normal Summon, and Nullgainer (+ Ultimaya Tzolkin) take Extra Deck slots. Your Spell starters already dodge most of those hand traps, so this adds starters that are easier to stop.

FTK

Combo (from the Centur-Ion FTK list on YGOPRODeck and Duel Evolution's FTK video):

  1. Bishop → Nullgainer → Witness, which places Bastion and Destrier.
  2. Destrier adds Opposition. Bastion places Shatranga, which adds Disturbance.
  3. Witness + Nullgainer → Ultimaya Tzolkin. Setting Opposition triggers Tzolkin, which summons Beelze of the Diabolic Dragons.
  4. Opposition puts Seneschal on their field and places Castellan. Seneschal lets you summon Cupid Pitch, and Castellan sends Brilliant Move to the GY.
  5. Cupid Pitch + the token → Mind Castlin. Brilliant Move from the GY → Enlisted. Enlisted + Mind Castlin → Scarlight Red Dragon Archfiend.
  6. Mind Castlin swaps Beelze onto their field. Scarlight destroys and burns.
  7. Their Beelze gains ATK each time they take damage. That's an effect they activate, so Destrier (placed) burns 500, which triggers Beelze again, and it loops until they're at 0.

Centur-Ion starters (Primera, Trudea, Stand Up) or Ascator can stand in for Bishop, since the combo only needs to reach Nullgainer.

Strengths

A turn-1 win from one card if nothing interrupts it. If the line is stopped after Witness, you still have a placed Angelechy board.

Weaknesses

Ash, Veiler, Ogre, Droll, Imperm, Nibiru or Harmonia each stop it. Duel Evolution's list mains only 3 Ash for interaction, so going 2nd is very weak. It needs 7–8 Extra Deck slots outside the Angelechy engine. And Beelze, Mind Castlin and Tzolkin are obvious ban targets: Flipside's July 2026 Deck Debut names them as the cards to hit.

Trif's rules (Oct 11 video), applied to you

  • Know the format first. Medius variants and the 60-card ROLAD pile are the decks that matter; Angelechy needs its own plan.
  • Going 2nd, go big: 24+ hand traps after siding, cutting up to ~8 engine cards. Mulcharmys count as half a play.
  • Think in pairs: Veiler + Nibiru, Veiler + Droll, Ash + Nibiru. Don't Veiler their first play: let it search, then Droll.
  • Pick your best 8 of ~10 side hand traps per matchup instead of fixed swaps.
  • Going 1st, add plays, not more negation. For you that's Boarder + Talent + Thrust; your Set Dominus traps are the negation.

Your templates

Going 1st: Fuwalos is dead (you'll control cards). Swap the 2 Ghost Ogre for 2 Thrust (your insurance against their hand traps), and bring in 3 Boarder + Talent; cut Nibiru where Boarder stops them reaching 5 summons; add the 3rd Thrust when Nibiru comes out too. When they hit you

  • Ash on a search (Strategy Score, Destrier, Shatranga, Disturbance): it's once per turn, so lead with the search you can afford to lose; Problem's summon and every placement can't be Ashed.
  • Impulse on Problem or Opposition: on turn 1 their GY has no Trap, so the card isn't destroyed. Switch to your other summon route; Bastion → Shatranga and Verdict's GY placement can't be Impulsed.
  • Droll: stop searching from the Deck. Shatranga can still add a Trap from the GY (discard Verdict to Problem first).
  • Ghost Ogre: destroys the card, but its effect still resolves. With Problem on the field, a destroyed Angelechy comes back as a summon.
  • Typhoon from hand: place Bastion before anything else you care about; it protects the others but not itself.

Under Fuwalos

Only Special Summons from your Deck or Extra Deck give them a draw: Enlisted (Problem), Seneschal (Opposition), Seneschal's optional extra summon, Opening to e4, Brilliant Move. Placements are free. Cheapest boards: 0 draws = Foolish Burial Goods → Verdict, then Verdict's GY effect (send an Angelechy card from hand) → Bastion → Shatranga (count 2). 1 draw = Problem's line without Opposition (count 3–4). 2 draws = the full Problem line, skipping Seneschal's extra summon (count 5, Verdict wipe).
