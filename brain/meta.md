## Tier lists and creator takes

- **Jesse Kotton (Oct 10).**
  - Driving force: Elfnote Ars Magna.
  - Strong contenders: the 60-card ROLAD pile, and Angelechy ("the best and most numerous hand trap lineup").
  - Decent: Fairy Tail, Power Patron/Artmage, pure Mitsurugi, Yummy.
  - Avoid: Kewl Tune, Branded, Sky Striker, Dark Time Wizard, Maliss.
- **DB Grinder (Oct 7).**
  - Best deck: Elfnote. Real arguments to play: Angelechy (23 hand traps) and ROLAD.
  - Format standard: 15+ hand traps in the main; Gallant Bandit out of main decks.
- **Trif, hand trap tier list (Oct 10).**
  - Fuwalos is great in every matchup. Nibiru is above Droll.
  - Purulia is bad vs Ars Magna.
  - Droll alone gets played through by Tinia, Regina and June Pride; pair it with "baby" hand traps.
  - Impulse and Purge are great, but Ars Magna decks can't run them.
- **Trif ("How to ACTUALLY beat Angelechy", Oct 12).**
  - The deck is "utterly broken going second" and "the best go-first stun deck".
  - High-impact cards: Impulse, Purge, Spark, e4, Ash, Ogre.
  - Keeping a "number one hand trap" against Angelechy secret for his paid masterclass.
- **Lucas Sacco (Oct 12):** ~80% win rate going second with 24 hand traps; "basically Tenpai with normal interaction".
