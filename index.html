    How Angelechy works

    Angelechy is a chess-themed Fairy deck whose monsters all live in the Extra Deck. Your Spells and Traps do one of two things with them: Special Summon them, or place them face-up in your Spell & Trap Zone as Continuous Spells. A placed Angelechy fires one effect when it lands (a search, a send to the GY, or placing the next piece), then keeps a continuous effect for as long as it stays. Every Angelechy Monster Card on the field, summoned or placed, yours or theirs, also counts for Angelechy Verdict, which banishes your opponent's whole field at 5 or more.

    Your 40 is half engine and half interaction. 19 engine cards build that board; 21 hand traps and Dominus Traps (24 interruptions counting Opening to e4) stop the opponent. Going first you build a board that limits what they can do. Going second you start building during their Standby Phase with Opening to e4 and spend hand traps on their key steps.

    Words this guide uses

    Placed
    An Angelechy monster in your Spell & Trap Zone as a face-up Continuous Spell. It still counts as an Angelechy Monster Card (for Verdict and Endgame Problem), but its effects are Spell effects: Ghost Ogre destroys it, yet its “when placed” effect still resolves, and Inspector Boarder doesn't count it as a monster type.
    The count
    Angelechy Monster Cards on the whole field: both players, Monster Zones and Spell & Trap Zones. Verdict reads it: 1–4 negates one card, 5+ banishes everything they control.
    Field Spells
    Angelechy Problem (your starter) and Angelechy Endgame Problem (the payoff Opening to e4 places on their turn). You can have one Field Spell; a new one sends the old one to the GY.
    Chess names
    Creators call Enlisted the pawn, Castellan the bishop, Bastion the rook, Shatranga the king and Destrier the knight. Opening to e4 is chess's most common first move.
    Starter
    A card that begins your combo by itself: Strategy Score, Problem, Terraforming, Opposition, Foolish Burial Goods. You run 13 (87.7% to open one going first).
    Spare
    Any Spell or Trap you can discard to Problem. Best: Verdict or Disturbance, because their GY effects still work afterwards.
    Hand trap
    A card you use from your hand during the opponent's turn: your monsters (Ash, Ghost Ogre, Fuwalos, Nibiru, Droll), the Dominus Traps, and Opening to e4 on their first Standby Phase.
    Dominus lock
    Activating a Dominus Trap from your hand stops you activating monsters of certain Attributes for the rest of the duel. A Set Dominus never locks anything. The lock table.
    Interruptions
    Cards that can stop the opponent. Going second you want 3 in your opening 6; with 24 that's 84% (Trif's minimum).

    What a finished board looks like

    Line A going first: Opposition gives them Seneschal and places Bastion, Bastion places Shatranga, Shatranga adds Verdict, and Problem adds Enlisted and Destrier. Seneschal's trigger lets you summon a second Destrier as a body.

    How a game goes

    Going first

    1. Build to 4+ placed pieces with Verdict Set and a Dominus or Opening to e4 behind it (line A or B).
    2. Place Bastion early against destruction (Typhoon, Spell Shattering Sword, The Fallen & The Virtuous): it protects the others, not itself.
    3. On their turn, let them commit, then Verdict at 5+. Ghost Ogre on a piece doesn't stop its “when placed” effect.
    4. After siding: Inspector Boarder first (lines E and F). With no Extra Deck monsters out, they can't activate monster hand traps at all.

    Going second

    1. Their Standby Phase: Fuwalos first (it needs you to control no cards), then Opening to e4. With Castellan placed you can loop into a 2nd e4 (line C, EG1).
    2. Their Main Phase: hand traps on their key steps (each matchup page lists them). Monster hand traps first, Dominus from hand last.
    3. Your turn: Verdict at 5+ banishes their field; Kusanagi or Black-Winged Assault Dragon finish (lines D and G).

    Mistakes every new Angelechy player makes

    1. Dominus from hand first. Impulse from hand locks Ghost Ogre, Nibiru, Veiler, Fuwalos and Droll for the rest of the duel. One replay lost to Elfnote this way.
    2. e4 before Fuwalos. Once you control a card, Fuwalos and Purulia can't be activated.
    3. Counting only your side for Verdict. The Seneschal you gave them counts too.
    4. Opposition into Bastion when you still need a Spell. Place Destrier to search instead (DB Grinder).
    5. Synchros in Attack Position against ROLAD. Dark Charmer crashes into them; summon in Defense.
    6. Forgetting Seneschal has 3,000 ATK. With Boarder out it can attack over Boarder; line F sends it back.
    7. Not reading their cards. Opponents misread this deck and concede early; read their cards just as carefully. DB Grinder: “read the cards once a week”.

    Main deck · engine (19)

    Angelechy Opposition×3Your best card: one card makes two Angelechy cards

    Normal Spell · 1 activation per turn

    What it does
    Choose 2 Angelechy monsters in your Extra Deck whose names aren't already in your Spell & Trap Zone. Special Summon one to your opponent's Main Monster Zone and place the other in your Spell & Trap Zone. From the GY, banish it to shuffle up to 5 Angelechy cards from your GY or banishment back into the Deck (Extra Deck monsters go back to the Extra Deck). The turn you activate it, you can only Special Summon LIGHT or DARK Synchros from the Extra Deck.
    Why you play it
    It starts a combo by itself and isn't a search, so Ash, Droll and Purge can't touch it; only Impulse stops it. You usually give them Seneschal. Its mandatory trigger lets you summon a Fairy Synchro too (a negated Destrier works as a body), it counts for Verdict from their side, and because it's their monster effect during your Main Phase it turns on Triple Tactics Thrust and Talent. The GY effect refills your single Verdict and Disturbance.
    Why 3
    It's the starter you most want to open, and a second copy is next turn's play. Never side it out.
    Tips
    Place Destrier (search a Spell) rather than Bastion when you still need a Spell later (DB Grinder). Against Elfnote, put Seneschal in their center Main Monster Zone: Regina only summons from the Deck from the center (Lucas Sacco). Impulse negates the whole effect, so make them spend it on something else first when you can.
    Angelechy Problem×3Main starter: any Spell or Trap becomes Enlisted plus a piece

    Field Spell

    What it does
    Once per turn: discard a Spell or Trap to Special Summon a Level 2 Angelechy (Enlisted) from your Extra Deck and place an Angelechy. Also, when a face-up Angelechy Monster Card you control is destroyed, you can return a placed Angelechy to the Extra Deck and then Special Summon it.
    Why you play it
    One card and a spare become two pieces: usually Enlisted plus Castellan (send Verdict or Disturbance) or Destrier (search a Spell). Opening to e4 places it from the Deck, and Strategy Score and Terraforming fetch it. The once per turn belongs to each copy, so a second Problem gets its own use (line B).
    Why 3
    It's the starter most of your hands use.
    Tips
    Discard Verdict or Disturbance when you can, because their GY effects still work. Otherwise discard Endgame, a sided Thrust, or a Dominus you won't Set.

    Foolish Burial Goods×3A starter through Disturbance or Verdict in the GY

    Normal Spell · 1 per turn

    What it does
    Send 1 Spell or Trap from your Deck to the GY.
    Why you play it
    Send Disturbance, then banish it to add any Angelechy Spell or Trap (Problem, Opposition, e4). Or send Verdict, whose GY effect sends an Angelechy card from your hand or field to place a piece.

    What it does

    Angelechy Opening to e4×3Your best card going 2nd: a board before they play

    Normal Trap · from your hand on their first Standby Phase

    What it does
    If your opponent goes first, you can activate it from your hand during the Standby Phase of their first turn. Place an Angelechy Field Spell from your hand, Deck or GY, Special Summon a Level 2 or 7 Angelechy (Enlisted, Castellan or Destrier) to the Extra Monster Zone, and place an Angelechy. Until the end of your next turn you can only Special Summon Synchros from the Extra Deck.
    Why you play it
    It builds before they act. Place Castellan and it starts the double e4: Castellan sends Verdict, Verdict places Bastion, Bastion places Shatranga, and Shatranga adds your 2nd e4, which isn't once per turn (line C, EG1). Going 1st, Set it as an interruption for their turn.

    Angelechy Verdict×1 +1 sideNegate at 1–4, banish their whole field at 5+

    Normal Trap · each effect once per turn

    What it does
    Counts the Angelechy Monster Cards on the whole field. 1–4: negate 1 face-up card they control. 5+: banish every card they control, without targeting. From the GY: banish it, send an Angelechy card from your hand or face-up field to the GY, then place an Angelechy with a name you don't already have placed.
    Why you play it
    It's your board's interruption and your way through theirs. The GY effect is a free placement: Castellan sends it, and you can chain it to put Bastion back when they Ghost Ogre or Typhoon your pieces (Lucas Sacco).
    Why 1
    Shatranga adds it from the Deck or GY, Castellan and Foolish Burial Goods send it, and Opposition shuffles a spent copy back. The 2nd copy is in your side for the mirror (“the god card in the mirror”, Trif).
    Tips
    Count both fields: the Seneschal you gave them counts. It doesn't target, so Magician of Dark Chaos (and Black Chaos while a Ritual Spell is in their GY) is unaffected, and Magician also stops their Spells and Traps being banished.
    Angelechy Disturbance×1Mostly a GY search for any Angelechy Spell or Trap

    Normal Trap · each effect once per turn

    What it does
    Give your opponent control of an Angelechy monster you control until the End Phase, then negate their face-up cards in the zones next to it. From the GY: banish it to add any Angelechy Spell or Trap except Disturbance.
    Why you play it
    The GY effect is what Foolish Burial Goods and Castellan want: it becomes whichever piece you need. Handing them Enlisted also triggers Enlisted's return: it goes back to your Extra Deck and you Special Summon any Angelechy (line D).
    Why 1
    Opposition recycles it. Trif: you go through Disturbance and Verdict on turn 1, then shuffle them back. Lists run 1–3. Never side out your only copy going 2nd.

    Angelechy Enlisted×3The pawn: the body Problem and e4 summon

    Synchro Tuner · Level 2 · LIGHT Fairy · 1500/1500

    What it does
    Target an opponent's monster in the next column: banish it, then move Enlisted to their Main Monster Zone. When its control changes, it returns to the Extra Deck and its owner (you) Special Summons any Angelechy from the Extra Deck. Each effect once per turn.
    Why you play it

    The double e4 summons two and Problem a third. Verdict's GY effect sends one to the GY, and the others return to the Extra Deck when their control changes.

    Hand traps & board breakers

    A hand trap is a card you use from your hand during your opponent's turn. Your 40 has 24 interruptions: 12 monster hand traps, 9 Dominus Traps and 3 Opening to e4. With 24 you open 3+ going second 84% of the time; most siding plans take you to 27 (93%). This page covers when to use each one, why you run that many and what would change the count. After that come hand traps outside your list, and the cards that break a board you couldn't stop.

    1. e4: place Problem from the Deck, summon Enlisted to the Extra Monster Zone, and place Castellan.
    2. Castellan placed: send Verdict from the Deck to the GY. This is the chokepoint — Ash, Ghost Ogre or Fallen & Virtuous here stops the loop, and Problem is still your field for your turn.
    3. Verdict (GY): banish it and send Enlisted from the field (that frees the Extra Monster Zone) → place Bastion → Bastion places Shatranga.
    4. Shatranga: add your 2nd e4. e4 isn't once per turn.
    5. 2nd e4: place Endgame Problem from the Deck (Problem goes to the GY), summon Enlisted to the Extra Monster Zone, and place Destrier → Destrier adds a Spell (Strategy Score or Opposition).
    6. Endgame: 4 placed → draw 1. It's still their Standby Phase.

    Their turn starts against: Castellan (one card or effect per chain), Shatranga (5 monster effects), Destrier (500 each time they activate something), Bastion (your other Angelechy cards can't be destroyed — Endgame included), and Endgame at 4 (you choose where they Special Summon; once per turn you banish what they Special Summon), with Destrier's search and the draw in hand. Lucas Sacco's version (Oct 12): the 1st e4 summons Destrier (Level 7) to the Extra Monster Zone instead of Enlisted, Verdict's cost sends that Destrier, and the 2nd e4 summons Enlisted and places your other Destrier — the same 4 placed. He also places Problem first and Endgame second, and runs one Endgame: a 2nd copy “is only relevant… when you open E4 and you're going second. Besides that, the card is a useless… brick.”

Keeping Endgame last also keeps its banish up all turn: when a player placed Endgame first and Problem second against ROLAD, Grinder pointed out that Super Starslayer TY-PHON “just gets banished” with Endgame at 4+; against Power Patron, Junoldo's negate-all Synchro is banished on summon. If they chain Fuwalos to your e4 (they control no cards yet), each Extra Deck summon draws them a card — Grinder still finishes the setup rather than stopping short.

    EG3 · Going 1st: Set e4, Endgame at their Standby (game 1)

    DB Grinder replays

    You end your turn with Problem as the field and e4 Set; at their Standby, e4 swaps the field to Endgame. Your going-1st siding takes Endgame out, so this is for game 1 (after siding, e4 places a Problem instead).

    1. Your turn (Grinder's correction on the Power Patron replay): Opposition → Seneschal to their field and place Destrier, not Bastion → Destrier adds Problem → Problem: discard → Enlisted + a placement. Set e4 and Verdict.
    2. Their Standby: flip e4 → place Endgame (Problem goes to the GY), summon Enlisted, place Bastion → Bastion places Shatranga → Shatranga adds Verdict.
    3. Endgame: draw 1.

    Grinder: “4 placed + Verdict set, field draw, more options”, and with Endgame at 4+ Junoldo's Synchro is banished on summon. e4 needs a free Spell & Trap Zone for its placement (it's still on the field while it resolves) and an open Extra Monster Zone.     EG4 · Your turn after line EG1: Verdict the turn you Set it

    From the card texts — not seen in a replay yet

    Endgame at 3+ lets you activate an Angelechy Trap the turn you Set it, and Verdict with 5+ Angelechy Monster Cards on the field banishes everything they control.

    1. After line EG1 you have 4 placed + Enlisted in the Extra Monster Zone = 5. Keep Endgame as your field — don't activate Problem yet.
    2. Strategy Score (Destrier's search) → add Verdict.
    3. Set Verdict and activate it now (Endgame's 3+ effect): banish all cards your opponent controls.
    4. Then Problem from your hand if you need more (it replaces Endgame), and attack.

    Needs a Verdict in the Deck: you run 1 (2 after siding against the mirror), and line EG1 banishes it — Opposition's GY effect can shuffle it back first. Count again before you commit: anything they removed lowers it, and at 1–4 Verdict only negates one card. Bastion keeps Endgame from being destroyed, not from Hey, Space Trunade! or a banish. Check the timing with a judge before relying on it.

    Playing Endgame well
    • One copy: Problem with the 1st e4, Endgame with the 2nd. Two copies: Endgame both times, drawing each time.
    • 2+ placed: you choose their summon zones — put their monsters in a column next to Castellan, whose banish targets a monster in its adjacent column. Against Elfnote, put Regina outside the center Main Monster Zone: her Deck summon only works from the center (Lucas Sacco).
    • 4+ is optional and once per turn: let a small summon through and banish the one that matters (TY-PHON, Junoldo's Synchro, their boss).
    • 5 placed: a free Angelechy from your Extra Deck every turn, but your Spell & Trap Zone is full, so no Set traps.
    • What beats it: Ghost Ogre or Ash on Castellan's send (the loop stops), Hey, Space Trunade! bouncing placed cards (Elfnote sides 2; a Mermail guide recommends it against you), Typhoon on Bastion first.

    Strengths

    More starters, and a fast route to 5 Angelechys for Verdict's banish-all. Witness can place from the GY as well, so Synchros that were destroyed come back as placements. It doesn't need Problem, so it plays through cards that answer Spell starters.

    FTK

    Combo (from the Centur-Ion FTK list on YGOPRODeck and Duel Evolution's FTK video):

    1. Bishop → Nullgainer → Witness, which places Bastion and Destrier.
    2. Destrier adds Opposition. Bastion places Shatranga, which adds Disturbance.
    3. Witness + Nullgainer → Ultimaya Tzolkin. Setting Opposition triggers Tzolkin, which summons Beelze of the Diabolic Dragons.
    4. Opposition puts Seneschal on their field and places Castellan. Seneschal lets you summon Cupid Pitch, and Castellan sends Brilliant Move to the GY.
    5. Cupid Pitch + the token → Mind Castlin. Brilliant Move from the GY → Enlisted. Enlisted + Mind Castlin → Scarlight Red Dragon Archfiend.
    6. Mind Castlin swaps Beelze onto their field. Scarlight destroys and burns.
    7. Their Beelze gains ATK each time they take damage. That's an effect they activate, so Destrier (placed) burns 500, which triggers Beelze again, and it loops until they're at 0.

    Centur-Ion starters (Primera, Trudea, Stand Up) or Ascator can stand in for Bishop, since the combo only needs to reach Nullgainer.

    Strengths

    A turn-1 win from one card if nothing interrupts it. If the line is stopped after Witness, you still have a placed Angelechy board.

    Weaknesses

    Ash, Veiler, Ogre, Droll, Imperm, Nibiru or Harmonia each stop it. Duel Evolution's list mains only 3 Ash for interaction, so going 2nd is very weak. It needs 7–8 Extra Deck slots outside the Angelechy engine. And Beelze, Mind Castlin and Tzolkin are obvious ban targets: Flipside's July 2026 Deck Debut names them as the cards to hit.

    When they hit you

    • Ash on a search (Strategy Score, Destrier, Shatranga, Disturbance): it's once per turn, so lead with the search you can afford to lose; Problem's summon and every placement can't be Ashed.
    • Impulse on Problem or Opposition: on turn 1 their GY has no Trap, so the card isn't destroyed. Switch to your other summon route; Bastion → Shatranga and Verdict's GY placement can't be Impulsed.
    • Droll: stop searching from the Deck. Shatranga can still add a Trap from the GY (discard Verdict to Problem first).
    • Ghost Ogre: destroys the card, but its effect still resolves. With Problem on the field, a destroyed Angelechy comes back as a summon.
    • Typhoon from hand: place Bastion before anything else you care about; it protects the others but not itself.

          How it counts
          • Jobs. Each main-deck card has a job you can change: Starter (makes a play by itself), Starter + a spare (needs another Spell or Trap to discard, like Angelechy Problem), Extender (continues a play), Interruption, Going-1st back-up (a second route with a starter, like Thrust or Boarder) and Brick. Known Angelechy cards start with the guide's roles, and cards whose text works from the hand on the opponent's turn start as interruptions.
          • A play: at least one starter, or a “starter + a spare” with another Spell or Trap.
          • Survives one hand trap: two plays, or a play plus an extender or a going-1st back-up. For Angelechy the guide's rules also count Problem with Verdict or Disturbance, and Foolish Burial Goods with Opposition, Strategy Score or Problem. Foolish Burial Goods only starts if the deck has a Disturbance or Verdict.
          • How exact: interruption odds are exact. Play odds come from 40,000 simulated hands, so they can move by about half a point.
