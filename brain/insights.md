# Insights — conclusions that took work, with their sources

⚖ = read from the card texts but worth confirming with a judge before relying on it at a sanctioned event.

## Rulings and interactions read from the card texts

**Engine**
- **Placed Angelechys** are Continuous Spells. They still count as "Angelechy Monster Cards" for Verdict and Endgame, but their effects are Spell effects: Ghost Ogre destroys the card while its "when placed" effect still resolves, and Inspector Boarder doesn't count them as a monster type.
- **Verdict** counts Angelechy Monster Cards on the *whole* field (both players, Monster and Spell & Trap Zones). 1–4: negate one face-up card. 5+: banish everything they control, without targeting. Its GY effect pays a banish **as a cost**, so Ghost Belle can't stop it; Skull Meister, D.D. Crow (when it's their turn) and Lancea can.
- **Opposition** summons one Angelechy to *their* Main Monster Zone and places another (names not already placed). Only Impulse stops it (not a search). Its GY effect shuffles up to 5 spent Angelechy cards back, so FBG's targets (Disturbance, Verdict) come back for later turns (the user's point, Oct 14).
- **Seneschal's summon trigger is mandatory and activated by its controller.** On their field it's *their* monster effect during your Main Phase, so it turns on Triple Tactics Thrust (any time that turn) and Talent (Main Phase), and Thrust can "add" because they control a monster. It also uses up their one effect under Boarder (line F). It fails if they used Purge from hand (DARK lock) ⚖.
- **Enlisted.** When its control changes it returns to the Extra Deck and its *owner* Special Summons any Angelechy; the return is activated by the new controller. If Enlisted targets Seneschal, Seneschal goes to the Extra Deck instead of being banished, so Enlisted never moves (line F) ⚖.
- **Opening to e4** works from the hand **only during the Standby Phase of their first turn**, when they went first. It summons a Level 2 or 7 (Enlisted, Castellan, Destrier) to the Extra Monster Zone, and only Synchros can come from the Extra Deck until the end of your next turn. On your own turn e4 is just a Set Trap, so don't search it then.
- **Endgame Problem thresholds (Angelechy cards in your Spell & Trap Zone):**
  - 1+: draw at their Standby.
  - 2+: you choose their summon zones (put Regina outside the center).
  - 3+: activate an Angelechy Trap the turn you Set it.
  - 4+: banish their Special Summons, once per turn.
  - 5: summon an Angelechy from the Extra Deck each turn.
- **Bastion** protects the *other* Angelechy cards from destruction by their effects. It doesn't protect itself, and banishing or bouncing (Hey, Space Trunade!) gets past it.
- **Castellan** (placed): each player gets only 1 card or effect per Chain, so they can't chain to your Nibiru (Lucas Sacco). Its send is the double-e4 loop's chokepoint (Ash).
- **Shatranga** (placed): they get at most 5 monster-effect attempts per turn. It adds a Trap from the Deck *or GY*, so Droll doesn't stop the GY add.
- **Destrier** (placed): 500 damage per activation of theirs. **Black-Winged Assault Dragon** deals 700 per monster effect, so the board with 2 Destriers placed + BWAD burns 1,700 per monster effect.
- **Kusanagi** gains the original ATK of its Synchro materials: Enlisted + Destrier = 3,000 + 4,000 = 7,000. It attacks every monster and deals piercing damage.
- **Synchro restrictions:** Strategy Score and Opposition allow only LIGHT/DARK Synchros from the Extra Deck that turn; e4 allows only Synchros. That's why there are no Link or Xyz pieces in the Extra.

**Hand traps and Dominus**
- **Dominus locks from the hand (rest of the duel):**
  - Impulse locks LIGHT/EARTH/WIND: Ogre, Nibiru, Veiler, Fuwalos, Droll, every Angelechy monster effect.
  - Purge locks DARK/WATER/FIRE: Ash, Purulia, Seneschal if you control it.
  - Spark locks EARTH/WATER/FIRE/WIND: Ash, Fuwalos, Droll, Purulia, but nothing LIGHT.
  - So use monster hand traps first and Impulse last. A **Set** Dominus never locks.
- **Spark:** from the hand only on a turn they've activated a monster effect in the hand or GY; a Set Spark has no condition. It targets. Then, **if you have no Trap in your GY, they may Special Summon from their hand**, so use it after a Trap (e4, Purge) is in your GY.
- **"You control no cards"** (Fuwalos, Purulia, Meowls; Imperm from hand; Evenly Matched from hand) and **"no Spells/Traps"** (Typhoon from hand), plus Lightning Storm's "no face-up cards", all die after your own Standby e4. Use them first.
- **Mulcharmy:** after one, you can activate only one other Mulcharmy that turn.
- **Droll** stops *both* players' adds from the Deck for the rest of the turn, draws included, so finish your Standby e4 searches first. Semi-Limited.
- **Inherent summons aren't effects.** Lucina, Tinia and Fortuna summoning themselves from the hand don't enable Spark and can't be Ashed. Regina's hand summon and Medius's GY summon *are* effects.
- **Inspector Boarder** counts monster **card types** on the field (Ritual, Fusion, Synchro, Xyz, Pendulum, Link), not races. At 0 types nobody can activate monster effects; each Enlisted or Seneschal raises the count.
- **Thrust's Set mode is not an "add"**, so it works under Droll, and their Droll turns Thrust on.

**Opponents' cards worth knowing**
- **Black Chaos** is unaffected by your activated effects *while they have a Ritual Spell in their GY*, targeting effects included. Answers: D.D. Crow on the Ritual Spell, or Lava Golem (a summon procedure, not an effect).
- **Magician of Dark Chaos – Black Chaos** is unaffected unless targeted, and their Spells/Traps can't be destroyed or banished. Verdict at 5+ doesn't hit it.
- **Hey, Space Trunade!** bounces 2 cards without targeting or destroying. Bounced Extra Deck cards go to the Extra Deck, so nothing goes back to the hand and nobody has to put cards on the bottom. Elfnote sides 2 against you.
- **Blitzclique** monsters' reveal effects summon from the hand, then lock them out of summoning Effect Monsters except from the hand for the rest of the turn.
- **TCG status (Oct 2026):**
  - Forbidden: Maxx "C", Dimension Shifter.
  - Limited: Terraforming, Called by the Grave, Crossout Designator, Harpie's Feather Duster, Bystial Magnamhut, Ext Ryzeal, Ryzeal Detonator.
  - Semi-Limited: Droll, Ice Ryzeal, Sword Ryzeal.
  - Unlimited: Raigeki.

## Matchup conclusions (details in the guide's deep dives and `matchup-research.md`)

- **Elfnote Ars Magna (most played).**
  - Best cards: Fuwalos, Ghost Ogre, Nibiru (Tyler Holland's MVPs).
  - Weak: one Droll or Ash alone (Tinia, Regina and June Pride play through them). Purulia is bad vs Ars Magna (Trif).
  - Going 1st Boarder is at its best (≈25 monster effects in their line).
  - Going 2nd: keep Spark; cut 2 FBG + Terraforming for 2 Veiler + 2nd Droll (27).
  - Put Seneschal (Opposition) or their summons (Endgame 2+) away from the center to blank Regina.
  - Every going-2nd Impulse comes from the hand and locks your best Elfnote cards, so play it last.
- **ROLAD pile.**
  - Droll shines (Trif). Purulia on Black Chaos, Souls and Kuriboh from the hand. Nibiru only before Dragoon.
  - Boarder is weakest here: their Ritual Spells reach a card type without a monster effect.
  - Black Chaos needs Crow, Lava Golem or a Kusanagi turn. Summon Synchros in Defense vs Dark Charmer.
- **Branded ROLAD.** Purulia is excellent. Nibiru is good (5+ summons). Black Chaos's 3,000 ATK loses to Kusanagi.
- **Mirror.**
  - Ghost Ogre is the best card; hit the first piece they place. The 2nd Verdict is "the god card" (Trif).
  - Typhoon their Bastion before you place anything. Nibiru, Veiler and Purulia are dead; Droll is weak.
  - Going 2nd Spark is mostly dead, so Thrust + Talent replace it.
- **Power Patron / Artmage.** Fuwalos and Droll hurt it most (DB Grinder). Purulia is bad (Ars Magna). Verdict or Spark answers Junoldo's negate-all Synchro.
- **Mitsurugi.** Ghost Ogre is bad (Saji is Tributed instead and searches). Droll is great. Impulse the Ritual.
- **Destiny HERO.** Never destroy Dark City at Midnight (it summons Dreadmaster), so no Ogre or Typhoon on it.
- **Dark Time Wizard.** Jinzo – Energy Shocker shuts off your Traps, so Spark comes out going 2nd.
- **Blitzclique.**
  - **Pure** (12 of 14 recent tops, Oct 14): Fuwalos is dead. Their reveal effects summon only from the hand and lock out other summons, and their full Extra Decks are Pot of Extravagance/Prosperity fodder (10 of 12 run the Pots).
  - **Ryzeal Blitzclique** (Rochester Top 4, Manchester Top 8, Sept 2026): Fuwalos draws. Ice Ryzeal summons from the Deck, and their Extra Deck is real Rank 4 Xyz (Ryzeal Detonator, Number 41 Bagooska, Evilswarm Exciton Knight, Number 60, Mereologic Aggregator).
  - The user says their locals play Ryzeal (Oct 14). Purulia and Impulse are the best cards either way; Spark is live all game.
- **Sky Striker.** Few hand traps in their main, so your turn usually resolves. Nibiru is dead; Spark comes out. Verdict at 5+ banishes their Set Quick-Plays.
- **Maliss.** Nibiru is strong. They main Impulse and Spark, so bait them. Lancea would be excellent but is not run.

## Rulings: draws count as adding from the Deck (Oct 15, user correction, checked)
- **Ash Blossom can negate draw effects, including Mulcharmy Fuwalos and Purulia.** Official ruling (Yugipedia, Card Rulings: Ash Blossom & Joyous Spring): Ash can be chained to Maxx "C"; Maxx "C" is then negated and draws nothing that turn. The Mulcharmies work the same way. So: your Ash stops their Fuwalos/Purulia on your turn, and their Ash can stop yours. Pairing a Mulcharmy with your Standby e4 does make them choose where Ash goes.
- **Draws also count for Droll & Lock Bird.** A draw outside the Draw Phase triggers Droll, and Droll's lock stops further draws that turn. Your Droll shuts off their Mulcharmy after its first draw (and stops your own searches, so search first). Their Droll during your Standby e4 loop also blocks Endgame Problem's draw.
- Also Ash-able for the same reason: Endgame Problem's Standby draw, Pot of Sloth, Triple Tactics Talent's draw-2 mode.
- (I first told the user Ash couldn't touch a Mulcharmy; that was wrong and was reverted the same day.)
