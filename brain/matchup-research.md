## Sacred Beast (Hamon/Uria/Raviel) — pre-BETB regional tops Sep 27 (Donnezac 2nd, Arapiraca T8)
Core: 3 Martyr, 1 Summoner, 3 Hamon, 1 Uria, 3 Raviel, 3 Released, 2 Fallen Paradise, 3 Thunderclap, 1 Combined Assault, Card of the Soul (some), 3 Ash, 3 Fuwalos, 2-3 Purulia, 2 Droll, 3 Belle; Fiendsmith / Super Poly variants.
Engine:
- Sacred Beasts Released (Normal Spell): add 3 Sacred Beasts from Deck, discard 2. GY (next turn): add Lv10.
- Martyr (Lv1 DARK Fiend): NS/SS -> place SB Field Spell or Continuous from Deck; if control another SB: SS 2 Martyr from hand/Deck/GY.
- Thunderclap (Continuous Spell): place 2 Thunderclap from hand/Deck/GY, reveal Lv10 -> place Fallen Paradise from Deck.
- Fallen Paradise (Field): up to 3x/turn send 3 same-type cards from hand/face-up field -> SS a Sacred Beast from hand/Deck/GY/banish (unaffected by opp activated S/T); draw 2 once/turn if control Lv10 SB.
- Hamon/Uria/Raviel reveal in hand: search SB Spell / Trap / monster then discard (monster effects in hand).
- Summoner (Lv8 LIGHT Thunder): reveal+discard -> SS SB from hand; discard -> SS from hand/GY; GY banish -> SS from GY.
- End board: Raviel (QE tribute 2 SB: destroy all opp monsters), Uria (QE destroy 1 S/T, no responses), Hamon (burn 1000 when monster sent to opp GY), The Chaotic Phantasmal Sacred Beasts (Fusion of 3 Lv10, 2x destruction protection, 3x/turn QE negate), Varudras (Rank 10 negate), Thunder Dragon Colossus (no adding from Deck except draw — hurts OUR Strategy Score/Destrier/Shatranga/Disturbance searches!).
Chokepoints:
- Ash: Released, any reveal search, Martyr's place-from-Deck? (place isn't add/SS/send — Ash does NOT hit place). Ash hits Martyr's SS-from-Deck (2 Martyr).
- Ghost Ogre: Fallen Paradise effect (face-up Field Spell activating) -> destroys the Field = engine dead; also Thunderclap (face-up Continuous) and on-field Martyr.
- Veiler: Martyr on field, Raviel/Chaotic on field. Not hand reveals.
- Droll: Released, reveals, Card of the Soul. Purge (hand): Released / reveals. Impulse: Fallen Paradise SS, Martyr SS, Summoner.
- Fuwalos: Fallen Paradise SS from Deck, Martyrs from Deck. Purulia: Martyr NS, SS from hand. Nibiru: Martyr x3 + SBs easily 5.
Vs Angelechy: their removal is destruction (Raviel monsters, Uria S/T) -> Bastion (placed) makes other Angelechy cards immune to opp destruction; Problem floats on destruction. Thunder Dragon Colossus shuts our Deck-to-hand adds (Strategy Score, Destrier place, Shatranga, Disturbance GY) — out it with Enlisted banish / Verdict negate / Disturbance.
Boarder (counts monster CARD types on the field: Ritual/Fusion/Synchro/Xyz/Pendulum/Link): every Sacred Beast is a main-deck Effect Monster -> 0 card types, so no reveals, no Martyr effects, no Raviel QE. Their Spells (Released, Thunderclap, Fallen Paradise) still resolve and Fallen Paradise can still put bodies on the field, but nothing activates until a Fusion/Xyz/Link (Chaotic Phantasmal via sending 3 Lv10s, Varudras Xyz, Links) or one of OUR Synchros (Seneschal on their field) adds a card type. Very strong.

## Maliss — Arapiraca regional 1st (Sep 27), Castleford T8, Chelmsford T4
Core: 3 Chessy Cat, 1 Dormouse, 1 White Rabbit, 3 March Hare, 3 Backup @Ignister, 1 Wizard @Ignister, 2 Underground (Field, semi), 1 Mirror, MTP-07, TB-11, Allure x2-3, Gold Sarc, Terraforming, Bystials, Dotscaper; non-engine: 3 Ash, 3 Fuwalos, Belle/Ogre, 2-3 Imperm, 3 Dominus Impulse, 2-3 Dominus Spark (!). Side: Purulia, Nibiru, Mistaken Arrest, Dimensional Fissure.
Engine: everything is DARK Cyberse. "If banished: pay 300 LP, SS it" (White Rabbit, Chessy, Dormouse); March Hare banished -> add a banished Maliss to hand.
- Underground (Field): on activation banish a Maliss from hand/Deck/GY (NOT an Ash target: banish ≠ add/SS/send). Dormouse ignition banishes from Deck. Chessy banishes from hand -> draw 2.
- White Rabbit summoned -> Set a Maliss Trap from Deck (activatable that turn by banishing a face-up Maliss): TB-11 (SS Maliss from Deck or Link from ED if opp has 3+ cards), MTP-07 (add Maliss + banish 1 card if they control a Maliss Link).
- Backup @Ignister NS -> search DARK Cyberse + discard. Wizard @Ignister revives.
- Links: Red Ransom (search Maliss Spell on SS; swaps opp ATK/DEF), Hearts Crypter (QE banish 1 card on field, unnegatable while pointing), Allied Code Talker (QE negate+banish by tributing a pointed Link), W:P Fancy Ball (QE negate+banish a monster effect on field/GY), Accesscode Talker (destroy), Firewall, Masquerena, S:P Little Knight.
Chokepoints:
- Ash: Backup search, Allure/Chessy draws, Red Ransom search, TB-11 SS from Deck, MTP-07 add. NOT Underground/Dormouse banishes.
- Veiler / Imperm / Ghost Ogre: White Rabbit on field when its Set trigger activates (stops the trap); Chessy/Dormouse on field.
- Droll: Backup, Red Ransom, MTP-07, Mirror GY add. Purge: same. Impulse: TB-11, Wizard, banished SS triggers.
- Fuwalos: tons of Extra Deck Link summons. Nibiru: easily 5 summons.
Vs Angelechy: their removal is BANISH (Hearts Crypter, MTP-07) -> Bastion protection does nothing; Opposition's GY effect shuffles banished Angelechys back. They pay LP constantly (300/900) -> Destrier placed (500 per activation) + Black-Winged Assault Dragon (700 per monster effect) burn adds up fast.
Going 1st with Boarder (counts card types: Link/Fusion/... on the field): main-deck Maliss are plain Effect Monsters -> at 0 card types White Rabbit/Chessy/Dormouse 'if banished' summons, Backup's search and March Hare are all locked. Underground still banishes, but the banish triggers can't activate. Their outs: Maliss TB-11 (Trap) summoning a Link from the Extra Deck if we control 3+ cards, or a Link Summon (no effect needed) -> 1 card type = 1 effect. Very strong. They main Dominus Impulse/Spark against our board (Impulse hits Problem/Opposition/e4/Brilliant Move SS effects) -> bait it with a lesser SS effect first.
Their main Dominus: Impulse negates our SS effects (Problem, Opposition, e4, Brilliant Move, Enlisted) from hand. Play around: bait with lesser SS effect first; Opposition is the big one.

Magical Meltdown's search is a Spell. Boarder slows them to 1–2 monster effects; their Fusion bodies (Mechaba, Caliga) still threaten.
